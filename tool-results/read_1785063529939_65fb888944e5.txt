    42→/**
    43→ * BlueprintChunkGenerator — a true algorithmically-independent chunk generator
    44→ * for Planet Suzaku whose surface terrain is derived from the
    45→ * {@link PlanetSuzakuBlueprint}'s canon geography (plus a tiny deterministic
    46→ * value-noise perturbation seeded by {@link DeterministicSeedHandler#CANON_SEED}),
    47→ * <em>not</em> from {@code minecraft:overworld} noise.
    48→ *
    49→ * <h2>CRON-COMPLETIONIST-60 — TRUE ALGORITHMIC INDEPENDENCE (point 10)</h2>
    50→ *
    51→ * <p>The CRON-71 design (this class's prior form) only wrapped
    52→ * {@link NoiseBasedChunkGenerator} and overrode {@link #getBaseHeight} to add
    53→ * canon terrain offsets on top of vanilla noise. The surface shape was still
    54→ * determined by {@code minecraft:overworld} noise — only nudged a few blocks
    55→ * per canon location. That was a "noise with offset" generator, not a "blueprint"
    56→ * generator. It failed the architectural goal of point 10: <i>"Terrain columns
    57→ * come from the blueprint+layers, not minecraft:overworld noise."</i>
    58→ *
    59→ * <p>This version (CRON-COMPLETIONIST-60) closes that gap:
    60→ * <ul>
    61→ *   <li>{@link #fillFromNoise} is overridden to fill each column from
    62→ *       {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)}, a pure deterministic function
    63→ *       of the blueprint's canon locations + {@link DeterministicSeedHandler#CANON_SEED}.</li>
    64→ *   <li>{@link #getBaseHeight} returns {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)}.</li>
    65→ *   <li>{@link #getBaseColumn} returns a column built from
    66→ *       {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)} — bedrock at the bottom, stone
    67→ *       up to the canon surface, water up to sea level, air above.</li>
    68→ * </ul>
    69→ *
    70→ * <p>The wrapped {@link NoiseBasedChunkGenerator} is still used for
    71→ * {@link #applyCarvers} (caves/ravines) and {@link #buildSurface} (grass/sand/
    72→ * snow/etc. biome surface rules). Those methods use vanilla's noise-derived
    73→ * carving and surface data, which is independent of the surface heightmap —
    74→ * they carve <i>through</i> whatever stone we placed, and apply surface rules
    75→ * on top of whatever stone surface we placed. This preserves caves, ores
    76→ * (ores are placed by feature decoration which queries existing stone), and
    77→ * biome surface variation for free.
    78→ *
    79→ * <p><b>Canon-driven surface formula:</b>
    80→ * <pre>{@code
    81→ *   canonSurfaceHeight(x, z) =
    82→ *       BASE_HEIGHT (64, one above sea level)
    83→ *     + sum_over_nearby_canon_locations(getTerrainWarpForLocation(loc)
    84→ *                                        * linearDecayFactor(distance, RADIUS=200))
    85→ *     + canonNoiseVariation(x, z)   // bilinear value noise, 8-block period,
    86→ *                                    // amplitude ±8, seeded by CANON_SEED
    87→ * }</pre>
    88→ *
    89→ * <h2>CRON-COMPLETIONIST-91 — BLUEPRINT+LAYERS INTEGRATION (point 10 full fidelity)</h2>
    90→ *
    91→ * <p>The CRON-60 design (this class's prior form) derived surface terrain from
    92→ * {@link PlanetSuzakuBlueprint} canon geography alone. That closed the
    93→ * "algorithmic independence from {@code minecraft:overworld} noise" gap, but
    94→ * left a deeper architectural promise unfulfilled: <i>"Terrain columns come
    95→ * from the blueprint+<b>layers</b>, not minecraft:overworld noise."</i>
    96→ *
    97→ * <p>The <b>layers</b> in that promise are the {@link CompositeWorldLayer}'s
    98→ * {@link WorldLayer}s — specifically the {@link Provenance#PLAYER} and
    99→ * {@link Provenance#SIMULATION} layers, which hold the journal of every
   100→ * block change since day 0. Before CRON-91, those layers were only consulted
   101→ * by {@link dev.ergenverse.runtime.materialize.PlanetSuzakuChunkMaterializer#onChunkLoad}
   102→ * — <b>deferred by 1 tick</b> to avoid mutating a chunk during its own assembly.
   103→ * The result: when a player walked back to a previously-edited area and the
   104→ * chunk regenerated from the canon base, there was a 1-tick window (50ms) where
   105→ * the player saw the unedited canon terrain <i>before</i> their PLAYER deltas
   106→ * were re-applied. A visible "flash of unedited terrain."
   107→ *
   108→ * <p>CRON-91 closes that gap. {@link #fillFromNoise} now does a <b>two-phase
   109→ * fill</b>:
   110→ * <ol>
   111→ *   <li><b>Phase 1 — canon base terrain.</b> Bedrock, stone up to
   112→ *       {@link #canonSurfaceHeight}, water up to {@link #SEA_LEVEL}, air above.
   113→ *       Unchanged from CRON-60.</li>
   114→ *   <li><b>Phase 2 — layer override.</b> If {@link WorldRuntime#get()} is
   115→ *       initialized, iterate {@link CompositeWorldLayer#layersInMaterializationOrder()}
   116→ *       and for each non-CANON layer, apply that layer's
   117→ *       {@link ChunkContribution#blockChanges} directly to the chunk via
   118→ *       {@link ChunkAccess#setBlockState}. This is safe during chunk-gen
   119→ *       (uses {@code isMoving=false}, no lighting/tick updates) and is
   120→ *       <b>idempotent</b> — {@code PlanetSuzakuChunkMaterializer.onChunkLoad}
   121→ *       will re-apply the same deltas 1 tick later as a safety net for the
   122→ *       chunk-from-disk reload path, where {@code fillFromNoise} does not fire.</li>
   123→ * </ol>
   124→ *
   125→ * <p><b>Why CANON structures are NOT built during {@code fillFromNoise}:</b>
   126→ * the {@link dev.ergenverse.runtime.materialize.StructureBuilderRegistry} builders
   127→ * need a live {@link net.minecraft.server.level.ServerLevel} (for entity spawns,
   128→ * heightmap resolution, etc.), but {@code fillFromNoise} only has a
   129→ * {@link ChunkAccess} (no level). CANON structure building stays in the
   130→ * materializer. PLAYER/SIMULATION block changes have no such dependency — they
   131→ * are pure (x, y, z, blockState) tuples — so they're safe to apply here.
   132→ *
   133→ * <p>The result: Heng Yue Mountain is always raised +30 near (4200, -1400),
   134→ * the Sea of Devils is always lowered −30 near (6000, -1184), Wang Family
   135→ * Village sits at +8 above sea level on a hill, etc. — every playthrough,
   136→ * regardless of vanilla noise seed. The hand-placed spawn at (3842, -1184)
   137→ * always lands on the same hill. This is <i>true algorithmic independence</i>
   138→ * from {@code minecraft:overworld} noise.
   139→ *
   140→ * <p><b>Codec &amp; registration:</b> Registered as {@code ergenverse:blueprint}
   141→ * via {@link ErgenverseChunkGenerators#BLUEPRINT}. The dimension JSON at
   142→ * {@code data/ergenverse/dimension/planet_suzaku.json} references the generator
   143→ * type as {@code ergenverse:blueprint} with {@code settings: minecraft:overworld}
   144→ * (the overworld settings are used for cave/surface-rule configuration, NOT for
   145→ * surface terrain shape — that comes from this generator).
   146→ *
   147→ * <p><b>Canon fidelity (fact-checked against 仙逆):</b>
   148→ * <ul>
   149→ *   <li>恒岳山 (Heng Yue Mountain): Zhao Country's largest mountain, raised +30.</li>
   150→ *   <li>修魔海 (Sea of Devils): vast perilous sea east of Zhao, lowered −30 → ocean.</li>
   151→ *   <li>朱雀墓 (Suzaku Tomb): underground inheritance site, surface depressed −12.</li>
   152→ *   <li>Wang Family Village: "赵国某偏僻小山村" — remote mountain village, raised +8.</li>
   153→ *   <li>藤家城 (Teng Family City): powerful family city in Zhao, raised +10.</li>
   154→ *   <li>雪域国 (Snow Domain): cold elevated country, raised +10.</li>
   155→ *   <li>恒岳派 / 炼魂宗 / 玄道宗 / 洛河门: mountain sects, raised +12 to +15.</li>
   156→ * </ul>
   157→ *
   158→ * <p><b>Known trade-offs (hyper-analytical self-critique):</b>
   159→ * <ul>
   160→ *   <li>The base noise variation is bilinear value noise (8-block period,
   161→ *       ±8 amplitude). This is intentionally crude — its purpose is to break
   162→ *       the perfectly-flat plateau that pure canon warping would produce
   163→ *       between canon locations, NOT to mimic natural terrain. Real terrain
   164→ *       variety now comes from biome-aware profiles (CRON-93) AND biome
   165→ *       surface rules (grass/sand/snow) applied by {@link #buildSurface}.</li>
   166→ *   <li>Caves are carved by vanilla carvers, which use vanilla noise to
   167→ *       determine WHERE to carve. Because our surface is canon-shaped (not
   168→ *       vanilla-noise-shaped), caves may occasionally carve into air (above
   169→ *       our surface where vanilla expected stone) or fail to carve (below our
   170→ *       surface where vanilla expected air). The net effect: caves still
   171→ *       exist but their density/surface-entrance distribution may differ
   172→ *       from a vanilla world. This is acceptable — the canon world has caves,
   173→ *       they just don't align with vanilla noise.</li>
   174→ *   <li>Ores are placed by feature decoration, which queries existing stone
   175→ *       blocks. Since we fill stone up to the canon surface, ores will be
   176→ *       placed normally. No loss here.</li>
   177→ *   <li>Aquifers (underground water) are part of vanilla noise generation.
   178→ *       Skipping vanilla {@code fillFromNoise} means we lose aquifers. This
   179→ *       is a known limitation; aquifers are mostly invisible to the player
   180→ *       (underground water pools) and the Sea of Devils already provides
   181→ *       surface water via our canon fill.</li>
   182→ *   <li>The wrapped generator's {@link NoiseBasedChunkGenerator#fillFromNoise}
   183→ *       is NOT called, so {@code chunk.getOrCreateNoiseChunk(...)} will be
   184→ *       lazily computed on first access (by {@code applyCarvers} or
   185→ *       {@code buildSurface}). This is fine — vanilla supports lazy
   186→ *       NoiseChunk computation.</li>
   187→ * </ul>
   188→ *
   189→ * <h2>CRON-COMPLETIONIST-93 — BIOME-AWARE TERRAIN PROFILES</h2>
   190→ *
   191→ * <p>The CRON-60/91 design derived surface height from canon geography +
   192→ * a flat ±8 fine noise, ignoring the biome entirely. A column in a
   193→ * {@code zhao_mountains} biome got the SAME surface height (Y=64 ± noise)
   194→ * as a column in a {@code zhao_plains} biome. This was a major visual
   195→ * regression from vanilla {@code minecraft:noise}, where mountains rise
   196→ * to Y=120+ and plains stay at Y=64. The world looked like a flat plateau
   197→ * with occasional canon-warped bumps — not a real cultivation world with
   198→ * mountains, plains, oceans, and valleys.
   199→ *
   200→ * <p>CRON-93 closes this gap by adding a {@link BiomeTerrainProfile}
   201→ * lookup to the height computation. The new
   202→ * {@link #biomeAwareSurfaceHeight(int, int, RandomState)} samples the biome
   203→ * source at (x, z) via {@link BiomeSource#getNoiseBiome}, looks up the
   204→ * profile for that biome, and uses the profile's base height + amplitude
   205→ * as the primary determinant of surface shape. Canon warps and fine noise
   206→ * still apply on top.
   207→ *
   208→ * <p><b>New formula (CRON-93):</b>
   209→ * <pre>{@code
   210→ *   biomeAwareSurfaceHeight(x, z) =
   211→ *       biomeProfile.baseHeight                  // plains=64, mountains=110, ocean=35
   212→ *     + biomeAmplitudeNoise(x, z, amplitude)     // ±amplitude, period 24
   213→ *     + canonTerrainOffset(x, z)                 // ±30 from canon locations
   214→ *     + canonNoiseVariation(x, z)                // ±8 fine noise (period 8)
   215→ *     clamped to [2, 256]
   216→ * }</pre>
   217→ *
   218→ * <p>The legacy static {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)} is RETAINED
   219→ * as a fallback for contexts where no {@link RandomState} is available
   220→ * (e.g., early chunk-gen race, non-Suzaku levels). It returns the
   221→ * pre-CRON-93 height (base 64 + canon warp + fine noise). Structure
   222→ * builders that have a {@link net.minecraft.server.level.ServerLevel}
   223→ * should call the new {@link #surfaceHeightFor(ServerLevel, int, int)}
   224→ * instead, which delegates to the biome-aware instance method.
   225→ *
   226→ * <p>MC 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.0 / Java 17.</p>
   227→ */
   228→public final class BlueprintChunkGenerator extends ChunkGenerator {
   229→
   230→    /**
   231→     * Codec for deserialization from the dimension JSON. Takes the same
   232→     * fields as {@code minecraft:noise} (biome_source + noise_settings)
   233→     * plus our registry key.
   234→     */
   235→    public static final Codec<BlueprintChunkGenerator> CODEC = RecordCodecBuilder.create(instance ->
   236→            instance.group(
   237→                    BiomeSource.CODEC.fieldOf("biome_source")
   238→                            .forGetter(gen -> gen.biomeSource),
   239→                    NoiseGeneratorSettings.CODEC.fieldOf("settings")
   240→                            .forGetter(gen -> gen.noiseSettings)
   241→            ).apply(instance, BlueprintChunkGenerator::new));
   242→
   243→    /** The noise generator settings holder (e.g. "minecraft:overworld"). */
   244→    private final Holder<NoiseGeneratorSettings> noiseSettings;
   245→
   246→    /**
   247→     * The wrapped NoiseBasedChunkGenerator that does the heavy lifting
   248→     * for caves (applyCarvers) and biome surface rules (buildSurface).
   249→     * <p><b>NOT used for {@code fillFromNoise}</b> — we override that
   250→     * fully to control surface terrain shape from canon geography.
   251→     */
   252→    private final NoiseBasedChunkGenerator wrapped;
   253→
   254→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   255→    //  CANON SURFACE CONSTANTS
   256→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   257→
   258→    /**
   259→     * Canon terrain warp radius — how far (in blocks) from a canon location
   260→     * the terrain is warped. Within this radius, the warp decays linearly.
   261→     */
   262→    private static final int TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS = 200;
   263→    private static final int TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS_SQ = TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS * TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS;
   264→
   265→    /**
   266→     * Maximum terrain warp magnitude in blocks. Positive = raise terrain
   267→     * (mountains). Negative = lower terrain (sea/tomb).
   268→     */
   269→    private static final int MAX_WARP_HEIGHT = 30;
   270→
   271→    /**
   272→     * Base surface height (one block above sea level 63). All canon warps
   273→     * and noise variation are added to this. Plains away from canon
   274→     * locations will sit at y=64 — dry land just above water.
   275→     */
   276→    private static final int BASE_SURFACE_HEIGHT = 64;
   277→
   278→    /**
   279→     * Sea level. Columns whose canon surface is below this fill with water
   280→     * up to sea level (oceans, seas, lakes). Vanilla overworld also uses 63.
   281→     */
   282→    private static final int SEA_LEVEL = 63;
   283→
   284→    /**
   285→     * Period of the bilinear value-noise variation, in blocks. 8 means
   286→     * smooth rolling hills on the scale of half-a-chunk — enough to break
   287→     * flatness between canon locations without producing noise-dominated
   288→     * terrain that would mask canon geography.
   289→     */
   290→    private static final int NOISE_PERIOD = 8;
   291→
   292→    /**
   293→     * Period of the CRON-93 biome-amplitude noise, in blocks. 24 means
   294→     * mountain-scale features (peaks/valleys every ~12 blocks, large enough
   295→     * to feel like real mountains, small enough to vary within a single
   296→     * chunk). Distinct from {@link #NOISE_PERIOD} (8) so the two noise layers
   297→     * produce decorrelated variation — biome amplitude gives the large-scale
   298→     * shape, fine noise gives the surface roughness.
   299→     */
   300→    static final int BIOME_NOISE_PERIOD = 24;
   301→
   302→    /**
   303→     * The quart Y used to sample the biome during chunk-gen. Biomes in
   304→     * {@code minecraft:multi_noise} are selected based on 4D parameters
   305→     * (temperature, humidity, continentalness, erosion, depth, weirdness)
   306→     * sampled at a quart position. Quart resolution is 4 blocks, so
   307→     * quartY = 16 corresponds to world Y = 64 (sea level). Sampling at
   308→     * sea level ensures we get the "surface" biome, not a cave biome or
   309→     * high-altitude variant.
   310→     */
   311→    private static final int BIOME_SAMPLE_QUART_Y = 16; // y=64 (sea level)
   312→
   313→    /**
   314→     * Amplitude of the canon value-noise variation. ±8 blocks gives gentle
   315→     * rolling hills; large enough to feel natural, small enough that canon
   316→     * warps (±30) dominate geography.
   317→     */
   318→    private static final int NOISE_AMPLITUDE = 8;
   319→
   320→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   321→    //  CONSTRUCTOR
   322→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   323→
   324→    private BlueprintChunkGenerator(BiomeSource biomeSource,
   325→                                     Holder<NoiseGeneratorSettings> noiseSettings) {
   326→        super(biomeSource);
   327→        this.noiseSettings = noiseSettings;
   328→        this.wrapped = new NoiseBasedChunkGenerator(biomeSource, noiseSettings);
   329→    }
   330→
   331→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   332→    //  CHUNK GENERATION — TRUE ALGORITHMIC INDEPENDENCE
   333→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   334→
   335→    @Override
   336→    protected Codec<? extends ChunkGenerator> codec() {
   337→        return CODEC;
   338→    }
   339→
   340→    /**
   341→     * Fill the chunk with canon-derived terrain.
   342→     *
   343→     * <p>This is the heart of the algorithmic independence: surface height
   344→     * per column comes from {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)}, NOT from
   345→     * vanilla noise. The wrapped {@link NoiseBasedChunkGenerator#fillFromNoise}
   346→     * is NOT called — we generate terrain entirely from canon geography.
   347→     *
   348→     * <p>Vanilla {@link #applyCarvers} and {@link #buildSurface} will lazily
   349→     * compute the {@code NoiseChunk} they need for cave placement and surface
   350→     * rules; those operations do NOT depend on the surface heightmap that
   351→     * {@code fillFromNoise} would have produced.
   352→     */
   353→    @Override
   354→    public CompletableFuture<ChunkAccess> fillFromNoise(Executor executor, Blender blender,
   355→                                                           RandomState randomState,
   356→                                                           StructureManager structureManager,
   357→                                                           ChunkAccess chunk) {
   358→        int minY = chunk.getMinBuildHeight();
   359→        int maxY = chunk.getMaxBuildHeight();
   360→        int minChunkX = chunk.getPos().getMinBlockX();
   361→        int minChunkZ = chunk.getPos().getMinBlockZ();
   362→
   363→        // ── Phase 1: canon base terrain (bedrock / stone / water / air) ──
   364→        // Cache block states — BlockState lookup is cheap but de-virtualized
   365→        // local refs are still marginally faster in a hot loop.
   366→        final BlockState stone = Blocks.STONE.defaultBlockState();
   367→        final BlockState water = Blocks.WATER.defaultBlockState();
   368→        final BlockState bedrock = Blocks.BEDROCK.defaultBlockState();
   369→
   370→        // MutableBlockPos avoids per-block allocation. setBlockState on a
   371→        // ProtoChunk during chunk-gen does NOT trigger lighting/scheduled
   372→        // tick updates — it just writes the section state.
   373→        BlockPos.MutableBlockPos pos = new BlockPos.MutableBlockPos();
   374→
   375→        for (int localX = 0; localX < 16; localX++) {
   376→            for (int localZ = 0; localZ < 16; localZ++) {
   377→                int worldX = minChunkX + localX;
   378→                int worldZ = minChunkZ + localZ;
   379→                // CRON-93: biome-aware height (mountains=110, plains=64,
   380→                // ocean=35, etc.) — replaces the flat canonSurfaceHeight.
   381→                // Falls back to legacy static if randomState is null.
   382→                int surfaceHeight = biomeAwareSurfaceHeight(worldX, worldZ, randomState);
   383→
   384→                for (int y = minY; y < maxY; y++) {
   385→                    BlockState state;
   386→                    if (y == minY) {
   387→                        state = bedrock;
   388→                    } else if (y < surfaceHeight) {
   389→                        state = stone;
   390→                    } else if (y < SEA_LEVEL) {
   391→                        state = water;
   392→                    } else {
   393→                        // Air — leave the section's default (air). Skip
   394→                        // setBlockState to avoid 65K useless writes per
   395→                        // column above the surface.
   396→                        continue;
   397→                    }
   398→
   399→                    pos.set(worldX, y, worldZ);
   400→                    chunk.setBlockState(pos, state, false);
   401→                }
   402→            }
   403→        }
   404→
   405→        // ── Phase 2: apply PLAYER + SIMULATION layer overrides (CRON-91) ──
   406→        // The architectural promise in point 10 is "terrain columns come from
   407→        // the blueprint+LAYERS" — not just the blueprint. Before CRON-91, the
   408→        // PLAYER/SIMULATION layers were only consulted by the chunk materializer
   409→        // on ChunkEvent.Load (deferred 1 tick), causing a visible flash of
   410→        // unedited canon terrain when a player walked back to a previously-edited
   411→        // area. Applying the layer overrides directly during fillFromNoise closes
   412→        // that 1-tick window: the player sees their edits immediately when the
   413→        // chunk first becomes visible.
   414→        //
   415→        // Safety:
   416→        //   - chunk.setBlockState(pos, state, false) is the SAME write path
   417→        //     vanilla's NoiseBasedChunkGenerator.fillFromNoise uses; it writes
   418→        //     the section state without triggering lighting/tick updates.
   419→        //   - This is NOT the same as level.setBlock(pos, state, UPDATE_ALL),
   420→        //     which is what the materializer defers to avoid. We are writing
   421→        //     to the ProtoChunk during its own assembly — exactly what
   422→        //     fillFromNoise is designed to do.
   423→        //   - Idempotent with the materializer: setting the same block to the
   424→        //     same state is a no-op. The materializer is still needed for the
   425→        //     chunk-from-disk reload path (where fillFromNoise does NOT fire).
   426→        //   - CANON structures (c.structures) are intentionally NOT built here
   427→        //     — StructureBuilderRegistry builders need a live ServerLevel, but
   428→        //     fillFromNoise only has a ChunkAccess. CANON stays in the materializer.
   429→        applyLayerOverrides(chunk, pos);
   430→
   431→        // Synchronous fill — return already-completed future. Vanilla's
   432→        // NoiseBasedChunkGenerator runs fillFromNoise on a worker thread via
   433→        // supplyAsync(executor); we don't need to because our fill is

   438→    /**
   439→     * Apply PLAYER and SIMULATION layer overrides to a chunk during fillFromNoise.
   440→     *
   441→     * <p>For each non-CANON layer in {@link CompositeWorldLayer#layersInMaterializationOrder()},
   442→     * queries {@link WorldLayer#getChunkContribution(int, int)} and applies any
   443→     * {@link BlockChangeDelta}s directly to the chunk via
   444→     * {@link ChunkAccess#setBlockState}. This is the CRON-91 upgrade that makes
   445→     * "terrain columns come from the blueprint+LAYERS" literally true — the
   446→     * layer journal is consulted during chunk-gen, not deferred to ChunkEvent.Load.
   447→     *
   448→     * <p><b>No-op when:</b>
   449→     * <ul>
   450→     *   <li>{@link WorldRuntime#get()} is not yet initialized (race during
   451→     *       server start — the runtime binds to the level on ServerStartingEvent,
   452→     *       which may fire AFTER the initial spawn chunks generate). In this
   453→     *       case, the materializer's onChunkLoad will apply the deltas 1 tick
   454→     *       later as a fallback.</li>
   455→     *   <li>The chunk has no PLAYER or SIMULATION deltas (the common case —
   456→     *       most chunks are unedited). The contribution is empty and the loop
   457→     *       is a near-noop.</li>
   458→     * </ul>
   459→     *
   460→     * @param chunk the chunk being assembled (Phase 1 base terrain already placed)
   461→     * @param pos   a reusable MutableBlockPos (avoids per-delta allocation)
   462→     */
   463→    private void applyLayerOverrides(ChunkAccess chunk, BlockPos.MutableBlockPos pos) {
   464→        // Defensive: WorldRuntime may not be initialized yet during the very
   465→        // first chunk-gen pass at server start. The materializer will catch up.
   466→        WorldRuntime runtime;
   467→        try {
   468→            runtime = WorldRuntime.get();
   469→            if (!runtime.isInitialized()) return;
   470→        } catch (Throwable t) {
   471→            Ergenverse.LOGGER.debug("[Ergenverse] BlueprintChunkGenerator: WorldRuntime unavailable during fillFromNoise — deferring layer overrides to materializer. Reason: {}",
   472→                    t.getMessage());
   473→            return;
   474→        }
   475→
   476→        CompositeWorldLayer worldLayer = runtime.worldLayer();
   477→        if (worldLayer == null) return;
   478→
   479→        int chunkX = chunk.getPos().x;
   480→        int chunkZ = chunk.getPos().z;
   481→
   482→        // Iterate in materialization order: CANON first → SIMULATION → PLAYER.
   483→        // PLAYER wins on conflict (applied last). CANON layer's c.structures
   484→        // are skipped (need a live ServerLevel — materializer handles those).
   485→        for (WorldLayer layer : worldLayer.layersInMaterializationOrder()) {
   486→            if (layer.provenance() == Provenance.CANON) continue;
   487→
   488→            ChunkContribution contribution;
   489→            try {
   490→                contribution = layer.getChunkContribution(chunkX, chunkZ);
   491→            } catch (Throwable t) {
   492→                Ergenverse.LOGGER.debug("[Ergenverse] BlueprintChunkGenerator: layer {} getChunkContribution failed for chunk ({},{}): {}",
   493→                        layer.provenance(), chunkX, chunkZ, t.getMessage());
   494→                continue;
   495→            }
   496→            if (contribution == null || contribution.isEmpty()) continue;
   497→
   498→            for (BlockChangeDelta delta : contribution.blockChanges) {
   499→                BlockState state = resolveBlockState(delta.blockState());
   500→                if (state == null) continue;
   501→                pos.set(delta.x(), delta.y(), delta.z());
   502→                chunk.setBlockState(pos, state, false);
   503→            }
   504→        }
   505→    }

   662→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   664→    /**
   665→     * Compute the biome-aware surface height at (worldX, worldZ), the
   666→     * authoritative height function used by {@link #fillFromNoise},
   667→     * {@link #getBaseHeight}, {@link #getBaseColumn}, and (via
   668→     * {@link #surfaceHeightFor}) structure builders.
   669→     *
   670→     * <p>Formula (CRON-93):
   671→     * <pre>{@code
   672→     *   biomeProfile.baseHeight                  // plains=64, mountains=110, ocean=35
   673→     *     + biomeAmplitudeNoise(x, z, amplitude) // ±amplitude, period 24
   674→     *     + canonTerrainOffset(x, z)             // ±30 from canon locations
   675→     *     + canonNoiseVariation(x, z)            // ±8 fine noise (period 8)
   676→     *     clamped to [2, 256]
   677→     * }</pre>
   678→     *
   679→     * <p>The biome is sampled at (worldX, worldZ) via
   680→     * {@link BiomeSource#getNoiseBiome} using the {@link RandomState}'s
   681→     * {@link RandomState#sampler() climate sampler}. The quart Y is
   682→     * {@link #BIOME_SAMPLE_QUART_Y} (sea level), ensuring we get the surface
   683→     * biome, not a cave or high-altitude variant.
   684→     *
   685→     * <p><b>Fallback behavior:</b> if {@code randomState} is {@code null}, or
   686→     * the biome lookup fails for any reason, this method falls back to the
   687→     * legacy {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)} (no biome awareness).
   688→     * This ensures robustness during edge cases (early chunk-gen race,
   689→     * non-Suzaku levels) at the cost of biome-blind height.
   690→     *
   691→     * @param worldX      the world X coordinate
   692→     * @param worldZ      the world Z coordinate
   693→     * @param randomState the world's random state (provides the climate sampler);
   694→     *                    may be {@code null} for fallback behavior
   695→     * @return the biome-aware surface height, clamped to [2, 256]
   696→     */
   697→    public int biomeAwareSurfaceHeight(int worldX, int worldZ, RandomState randomState) {
   698→        // Fallback: no randomState → use legacy static (no biome awareness)
   699→        if (randomState == null) {
   700→            return canonSurfaceHeight(worldX, worldZ);
   701→        }
   702→
   703→        BiomeTerrainProfile profile = sampleBiomeProfile(worldX, worldZ, randomState);
   704→        int offset = getCanonTerrainOffset(worldX, worldZ);
   705→        int fineNoise = canonNoiseVariation(worldX, worldZ);
   706→        int biomeNoise = biomeAmplitudeNoise(worldX, worldZ, profile.amplitude());
   707→
   708→        int h = profile.baseHeight() + biomeNoise + offset + fineNoise;
   709→        // Clamp: same [2, 256] bounds as canonSurfaceHeight
   710→        if (h < 2) return 2;
   711→        if (h > 256) return 256;
   712→        return h;
   713→    }

   715→    /**
   716→     * Sample the biome at (worldX, worldZ) and return its terrain profile.
   717→     *
   718→     * <p>Uses {@link BiomeSource#getNoiseBiome} with the climate sampler from
   719→     * {@code randomState}. The quart Y is {@link #BIOME_SAMPLE_QUART_Y}
   720→     * (sea level). Returns {@link BiomeTerrainProfile#DEFAULT} if the lookup
   721→     * fails for any reason (defensive — should not occur in practice).
   722→     */
   723→    private BiomeTerrainProfile sampleBiomeProfile(int worldX, int worldZ, RandomState randomState) {
   724→        try {
   725→            int quartX = worldX >> 2;
   726→            int quartZ = worldZ >> 2;
   727→            // In 1.20.1 Mojmaps, RandomState.sampler() returns the Climate.Sampler
   728→            // used by the multi-noise biome source. Quart resolution is 4 blocks;
   729→            // quartY = 16 corresponds to world Y = 64 (sea level) — see BIOME_SAMPLE_QUART_Y.
   730→            Climate.Sampler sampler = randomState.sampler();
   731→            Holder<Biome> biome = biomeSource.getNoiseBiome(quartX, BIOME_SAMPLE_QUART_Y, quartZ, sampler);
   732→            if (biome == null) return BiomeTerrainProfile.DEFAULT;
   733→            return biome.unwrapKey()
   734→                    .map(key -> BiomeTerrainProfile.forBiome(key.location()))
   735→                    .orElse(BiomeTerrainProfile.DEFAULT);
   736→        } catch (Throwable t) {
   737→            Ergenverse.LOGGER.debug("[Ergenverse] BlueprintChunkGenerator: biome sample failed at ({},{}): {}",
   738→                    worldX, worldZ, t.getMessage());
   739→            return BiomeTerrainProfile.DEFAULT;
   740→        }
   741→    }

   743→    /**
   744→     * Get the biome-aware surface height at (worldX, worldZ) for the given
   745→     * {@link ServerLevel}. This is the entry point structure builders
   746→     * should use (CRON-93).
   747→     *
   748→     * <p>If the level's chunk generator is a {@link BlueprintChunkGenerator},
   749→     * this delegates to {@link #biomeAwareSurfaceHeight} with the level's
   750→     * {@link RandomState}. Otherwise (e.g., non-Suzaku levels, fallback
   751→     * generators), it falls back to the legacy {@link #canonSurfaceHeight}
   752→     * (no biome awareness).
   753→     *
   754→     * <p><b>Migration guide (CRON-93):</b> structure builders should replace
   755→     * calls to the static {@code canonSurfaceHeight(x, z)} with
   756→     * {@code surfaceHeightFor(level, x, z)}. Both return the same value when
   757→     * the level is Suzaku, but the new method accounts for the biome at
   758→     * (x, z), producing canon-faithful elevations (mountains at Y=110,
   759→     * plains at Y=64, oceans at Y=35, etc.).
   760→     *
   761→     * <pre>{@code
   762→     *   // BEFORE (CRON-67, no biome awareness):
   763→     *   int surfaceY = BlueprintChunkGenerator.canonSurfaceHeight(SECT_X, SECT_Z);
   764→     *
   765→     *   // AFTER (CRON-93, biome-aware):
   766→     *   int surfaceY = BlueprintChunkGenerator.surfaceHeightFor(level, SECT_X, SECT_Z);
   767→     * }</pre>
   768→     *
   769→     * @param level  the server level (must be Planet Suzaku for biome-aware
   770→     *               results; other levels fall back to legacy behavior)
   771→     * @param worldX the world X coordinate
   772→     * @param worldZ the world Z coordinate

   794→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   796→    /**
   797→     * Compute the canon-derived surface height at (worldX, worldZ) — the
   798→     * <b>legacy, biome-blind</b> version.
   799→     *
   800→     * <p>{@code BASE_SURFACE_HEIGHT + canonTerrainOffset + canonNoiseVariation},
   801→     * clamped to a non-negative minimum (bedrock layer at {@code minY} must
   802→     * always have at least one stone block above it).
   803→     *
   804→     * <p><b>CRON-COMPLETIONIST-93:</b> this method is RETAINED as a fallback
   805→     * for contexts where no {@link RandomState} or {@link ServerLevel} is
   806→     * available (e.g., early chunk-gen race, non-Suzaku levels, defensive
   807→     * code paths). For all structure-builder contexts where a
   808→     * {@link ServerLevel} is available, prefer
   809→     * {@link #surfaceHeightFor(ServerLevel, int, int)} — it accounts for
   810→     * the biome at (x, z) and produces canon-faithful elevations.
   811→     *
   812→     * <p><b>CRON-COMPLETIONIST-67:</b> this method is {@code public} so that
   813→     * structure builders can resolve their center Y from the <i>same canon
   814→     * authority</i> that the chunk generator uses — eliminating the heightmap
   815→     * race condition where {@code level.getHeightmapPos(MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES, ...)}
   816→     * would return y=0 or a stale value if the chunk at the canon center wasn't
   817→     * loaded yet when {@code buildForChunk} fired for an adjacent chunk. The
   818→     * canon surface height is a pure deterministic function of (x, z) — it does
   819→     * NOT depend on chunk-load state, so it returns the correct Y every time,
   820→     * regardless of which chunks are loaded.
   821→     *
   822→     * <p>For contexts without a {@link ServerLevel} (e.g., static helpers,
   823→     * tests), continue to call this directly:
   824→     * <pre>{@code
   825→     *   int surfaceY = BlueprintChunkGenerator.canonSurfaceHeight(SECT_X, SECT_Z);
   826→     * }</pre>
   827→     *
   828→     * For contexts WITH a {@link ServerLevel}, prefer the biome-aware version:
   829→     * <pre>{@code
   830→     *   int surfaceY = BlueprintChunkGenerator.surfaceHeightFor(level, SECT_X, SECT_Z);
   831→     * }</pre>
   832→     */
   833→    public static int canonSurfaceHeight(int worldX, int worldZ) {
   834→        int offset = getCanonTerrainOffset(worldX, worldZ);
   835→        int noise = canonNoiseVariation(worldX, worldZ);
   836→        int h = BASE_SURFACE_HEIGHT + offset + noise;
   837→        // Clamp: never let the surface drop to or below 1 (need at least
   838→        // bedrock + stone). Also never exceed a sane cap to avoid Y=320
   839→        // pillars on canon warps (current max warp is +30, so the cap is
   840→        // mostly a defensive measure).
   841→        if (h < 2) return 2;
   842→        if (h > 256) return 256;
   843→        return h;
   844→    }

   846→    /**
   847→     * Compute the biome-amplitude noise at (worldX, worldZ) — a bilinear
   848→     * value noise with period {@link #BIOME_NOISE_PERIOD} and the given
   849→     * amplitude. Used by {@link #biomeAwareSurfaceHeight} to produce
   850→     * biome-scale terrain variation (mountain peaks, valley undulations).
   851→     *
   852→     * <p>Returns 0 when {@code amplitude <= 0} (no variation needed for
   853→     * flat biomes like plains, though plains still use amplitude 4 for
   854→     * gentle rolling).
   855→     *
   856→     * <p>The hash function is the same splitmix64 mixer used by
   857→     * {@link #canonNoiseVariation}, but with a different cell period (24
   858→     * vs 8), so the two noise layers are decorrelated. Both are seeded by
   859→     * {@link DeterministicSeedHandler#CANON_SEED}, ensuring determinism.
   860→     */
   861→    static int biomeAmplitudeNoise(int worldX, int worldZ, int amplitude) {
   862→        if (amplitude <= 0) return 0;
   863→        int cellX = Math.floorDiv(worldX, BIOME_NOISE_PERIOD);
   864→        int cellZ = Math.floorDiv(worldZ, BIOME_NOISE_PERIOD);
   865→        int fracX = worldX - cellX * BIOME_NOISE_PERIOD;
   866→        int fracZ = worldZ - cellZ * BIOME_NOISE_PERIOD;
   867→
   868→        // Smoothstep interpolation weights
   869→        double sx = (double) fracX / BIOME_NOISE_PERIOD;
   870→        double sz = (double) fracZ / BIOME_NOISE_PERIOD;
   871→        sx = sx * sx * (3 - 2 * sx);
   872→        sz = sz * sz * (3 - 2 * sz);
   873→
   874→        // Four corner values in range [0, 2*amplitude]
   875→        double v00 = amplitudeHash(cellX,     cellZ,     amplitude);
   876→        double v10 = amplitudeHash(cellX + 1, cellZ,     amplitude);
   877→        double v01 = amplitudeHash(cellX,     cellZ + 1, amplitude);
   878→        double v11 = amplitudeHash(cellX + 1, cellZ + 1, amplitude);
   879→
   880→        double ix0 = v00 + (v10 - v00) * sx;
   881→        double ix1 = v01 + (v11 - v01) * sx;
   882→        double val = ix0 + (ix1 - ix0) * sz;
   883→
   884→        // Center around zero: range [-amplitude, +amplitude]
   885→        return (int) Math.round(val - amplitude);
   886→    }

   888→    /**
   889→     * Salt value mixed into the amplitude-noise hash to decorrelate it from
   890→     * the fine-noise hash (so the two noise layers don't produce correlated
   891→     * values). Arbitrary 64-bit constant; chosen to be distinct from any
   892→     * multiplier used in {@link #noiseHash}.
   893→     */
   894→    private static final long AMPLITUDE_HASH_SALT = 0xA5A5A5A5A5A5A5A5L;
   895→
   896→    /**
   897→     * Deterministic hash for the biome-amplitude noise → value in
   898→     * [0, 2 * amplitude]. Same splitmix64 mixing as {@link #noiseHash} but
   899→     * parameterized by amplitude (so different amplitudes produce different
   900→     * value ranges). Uses {@link #AMPLITUDE_HASH_SALT} to decorrelate from
   901→     * the fine noise (otherwise the two noise layers would be correlated,
   902→     * producing unrealistic terrain).
   903→     */
   904→    private static long amplitudeHash(int cellX, int cellZ, int amplitude) {
   905→        long h = DeterministicSeedHandler.CANON_SEED ^ AMPLITUDE_HASH_SALT;
   906→        h ^= (long) cellX * 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15L;
   907→        h ^= (long) cellZ * 0xC2B2AE3D27D4EB4FL;
   908→        h ^= h >>> 33;
   909→        h *= 0xFF51AFD7ED558CCDL;
   910→        h ^= h >>> 33;
   911→        h *= 0xC4CEB9FE1A85EC53L;
   912→        h ^= h >>> 33;
   913→        return (h & 0xFFFFFFFFL) % (2L * amplitude + 1);
   914→    }
   915→
   916→    /**
   917→     * Compute the canon terrain offset at (worldX, worldZ).
   918→     * Returns the sum of all nearby canon location influences.
   919→     * Positive = raise terrain (mountain), Negative = lower terrain (sea/tomb).
   920→     *
   921→     * <p>Each canon location has an influence that decays linearly from its
   922→     * center to {@link #TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS} blocks away. The offset is
   923→     * deterministic and based on the blueprint's geography.
   924→     */
   925→    static int getCanonTerrainOffset(int worldX, int worldZ) {
   926→        PlanetSuzakuBlueprint blueprint = PlanetSuzakuBlueprint.canonical();
   927→        int totalOffset = 0;
   928→
   929→        for (PlanetSuzakuBlueprint.CanonLocation loc : blueprint.allLocations().values()) {
   930→            int dx = worldX - loc.x;
   931→            int dz = worldZ - loc.z;
   932→            int distSq = dx * dx + dz * dz;
   933→
   934→            if (distSq >= TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS_SQ) continue;
   935→
   936→            double dist = Math.sqrt(distSq);
   937→            double factor = 1.0 - (dist / TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS);
   938→
   939→            int warp = getTerrainWarpForLocation(loc);
   940→            totalOffset += (int) (warp * factor);
   941→        }
   942→
   943→        return totalOffset;
   944→    }
   945→
   946→    /**
   947→     * Compute a small deterministic value-noise variation at (worldX, worldZ).
   948→     * Bilinear interpolation of a per-cell hash, scaled to ±{@link #NOISE_AMPLITUDE}.
   949→     * Period = {@link #NOISE_PERIOD} blocks.
   950→     *
   951→     * <p>The hash is seeded by {@link DeterministicSeedHandler#CANON_SEED},
   952→     * so the variation is identical every save. The purpose of this noise is
   953→     * NOT to mimic natural terrain — it is to break the perfectly-flat plateau
   954→     * that pure canon warping would produce between canon locations.
   955→     */
   956→    static int canonNoiseVariation(int worldX, int worldZ) {
   957→        int cellX = Math.floorDiv(worldX, NOISE_PERIOD);
   958→        int cellZ = Math.floorDiv(worldZ, NOISE_PERIOD);
   959→        int fracX = worldX - cellX * NOISE_PERIOD;
   960→        int fracZ = worldZ - cellZ * NOISE_PERIOD;
   961→
   962→        // Smoothstep interpolation weights
   963→        double sx = (double) fracX / NOISE_PERIOD;
   964→        double sz = (double) fracZ / NOISE_PERIOD;
   965→        sx = sx * sx * (3 - 2 * sx);
   966→        sz = sz * sz * (3 - 2 * sz);
   967→
   968→        // Four corner values in range [0, 2*NOISE_AMPLITUDE]
   969→        double v00 = noiseHash(cellX,     cellZ);
   970→        double v10 = noiseHash(cellX + 1, cellZ);
   971→        double v01 = noiseHash(cellX,     cellZ + 1);
   972→        double v11 = noiseHash(cellX + 1, cellZ + 1);
   973→
   974→        double ix0 = v00 + (v10 - v00) * sx;
   975→        double ix1 = v01 + (v11 - v01) * sx;
   976→        double val = ix0 + (ix1 - ix0) * sz;
   977→
   978→        // Center around zero: range [-NOISE_AMPLITUDE, +NOISE_AMPLITUDE]
   979→        return (int) Math.round(val - NOISE_AMPLITUDE);
   980→    }
   981→
   982→    /**
   983→     * Deterministic hash → value in [0, 2 * NOISE_AMPLITUDE]. Uses a
   984→     * splitmix64-style mixing of (cellX, cellZ, CANON_SEED) so that
   985→     * adjacent cells produce decorrelated values.
   986→     */
   987→    private static long noiseHash(int cellX, int cellZ) {
   988→        long h = DeterministicSeedHandler.CANON_SEED;
   989→        h ^= (long) cellX * 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15L;
   990→        h ^= (long) cellZ * 0xC2B2AE3D27D4EB4FL;
   991→        h ^= h >>> 33;
   992→        h *= 0xFF51AFD7ED558CCDL;
   993→        h ^= h >>> 33;
   994→        h *= 0xC4CEB9FE1A85EC53L;
   995→        h ^= h >>> 33;
   996→        // Map to [0, 2 * NOISE_AMPLITUDE]
   997→        return (h & 0xFFFFFFFFL) % (2L * NOISE_AMPLITUDE + 1);
   998→    }

  1000→    /**
  1001→     * Get the terrain warp height for a canon location.
  1002→     * Positive = raise (mountains), Negative = lower (seas/tombs).
  1003→     *
  1004→     * <p><b>Canon fidelity (fact-checked against 仙逆):</b>
  1005→     * <ul>
  1006→     *   <li>恒岳山 (Heng Yue Mountain): the mountain where Heng Yue Sect is
  1007→     *       located. Canon describes it as Zhao Country's largest mountain.
  1008→     *       Raised terrain is correct.</li>
  1009→     *   <li>修魔海 (Sea of Devils): a vast, perilous sea east of Zhao Country.
  1010→     *       Lowered terrain (below sea level) creates ocean.</li>
  1011→     *   <li>朱雀墓 (Suzaku Tomb): underground inheritance site. The entrance
  1012→     *       is on the surface but leads underground. Slight depression is
  1013→     *       appropriate for the entrance area.</li>
  1014→     *   <li>Wang Family Village: "赵国某偏僻小山村" — a remote mountain village.
  1015→     *       Slight elevation fits the "mountain" description.</li>
  1016→     *   <li>藤家城 (Teng Family City): powerful family city in Zhao Country.
  1017→     *       Moderate elevation for a walled city.</li>

  1019→     */
  1020→    private static int getTerrainWarpForLocation(PlanetSuzakuBlueprint.CanonLocation loc) {
