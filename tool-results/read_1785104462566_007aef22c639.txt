 11578→  1. **The wiring is correct but only one consumer exists.** CultivatorMeditationGoal is the ONLY AI goal that queries AnchorRegistryService. The user's directive envisions a broader pipeline: "Find Wang Lin → Find House → Find Bedroom → Find Bed → Compiler Anchor → Navigation Target." Each is a separate goal enhancement. Score 6/10 for breadth (one consumer out of ~14 possible anchor roles).
 11579→  2. **The settlement-id-to-faction mapping is a convention, not enforced.** The code assumes cultivator.getSectId() equals the canon location id (e.g. "wang_family_village" faction → "wang_family_village" settlement id). This holds for Wang Family Village NPCs (their JSON has "faction": "wang_family_village") but may not hold for all NPCs. An NPC with "faction": "wang_family" (no "_village") would fail to find the registry. The findNearestGlobal fallback covers this, but it's inelegant. Score 5/10 for robustness (convention works for current data, but no validation).
 11580→  3. **The 64-block max distance is arbitrary.** A cultivator at the village edge might be 80 blocks from Wang Lin's meditation mat and fail to find it (falls back to in-place meditation). The 64-block limit was chosen as "reasonable walking distance" but isn't canon-derived. A cultivator should walk across the entire village (which is ~83 blocks across) to reach their meditation spot. Score 6/10 for distance tuning (should probably be 128 or settlement-bounded).
 11581→  4. **The seek timeout falls back silently.** If a cultivator gets stuck (blocked path, unloaded chunk between them and the anchor), after 30s they give up and meditate in place. This is correct behavior (don't hang the AI) but the player sees a cultivator suddenly stop walking and meditate in a random spot. Score 7/10 for graceful degradation (correct, but visually jarring).
 11582→  5. **No path validation.** The cultivator uses vanilla navigation.moveTo, which uses Minecraft's pathfinder. If the meditation mat is on a different floor (e.g. second story), the pathfinder may not find a path (no stairs registered as walkable). The cultivator would walk to the base of the building and time out. Score 5/10 for multi-floor navigation (architectural limitation — needs custom pathfinder or stair-aware navigation).
 11583→  6. **The AnchorRegistry is not persisted across save/load.** On world reload, AnchorRegistryService starts empty. It only gets populated when CanonSettlementBuilder runs again (on next chunk-load materialization of Wang Family Village). Score 7/10 for persistence gap (acceptable, documented in AnchorRegistryService Javadoc).

 11632→- HARSH SELF-CRITIQUE (hyper-analytical, fact-checked against canon):
 11633→  1. **No visible sword-under-feet ModelPart.** The flight pose implies the sword (body lean, swept arms, robe billow) but does not render a visible sword under the cultivator's feet. The held-weapon sword (swordRight) is hidden during flight. A future CRON should add a dedicated `ride_sword` ModelPart as a child of the body, positioned at y=-1.5 (below the feet), visible only when POSE_FLYING. This would render a horizontal sword under the cultivator — the iconic visual. Score 6/10 for visual completeness (pose is right, prop is missing).
 11634→  2. **Flight uses direct velocity manipulation, not a custom FlightPathNavigator.** Direct setDeltaMovement works for straight-line pursuit but cannot path around obstacles (mountains, trees, walls). A Foundation+ cultivator flying toward a far target will fly THROUGH trees and walls (no collision check during flight). The cultivator can fly through a mountain — visually jarring and not canon-faithful (cultivators in the novel fly around mountains, not through them). A future CRON should implement a FlightPathNavigator that does ray-cast obstacle detection and curves the flight path around obstacles. Score 5/10 for pathfinding realism (works for open terrain, fails for complex terrain).
 11635→  3. **No collision/impact handling on landing.** When flight stops (target within 8 blocks or timeout), the cultivator's velocity is zeroed and gravity is restored. The cultivator drops to the surface. If the cultivator was flying at altitude 20 and stops above a tree, they will land on the tree leaves (no fall damage because they're a Mob, but visually awkward). A future CRON should detect the surface Y below the cultivator at stop time and descend gradually rather than dropping. Score 6/10 for landing behavior (functional but ungraceful).
 11636→  4. **The 30-second timeout is arbitrary.** A cultivator pursuing a far target (e.g., 200 blocks away) at 0.4 blocks/tick = 8 blocks/sec would take 25 seconds to arrive. The 30s timeout leaves only 5s margin. If the cultivator has to climb (ascend to cruise altitude first), the effective travel time is longer. The timeout may fire before the cultivator arrives, causing them to land and walk the rest of the way. Score 6/10 for timeout tuning (should be 60s or scaled to distance).
 11637→  5. **No qi expenditure modeling.** Canon: sword flight consumes qi; a cultivator at low qi cannot fly indefinitely. The current implementation has no qi cost — a cultivator can fly forever (within the 30s timeout). A future CRON should query the cultivator's qi reserves and refuse flight activation (or abort mid-flight) when qi is below a threshold. Score 4/10 for canon-faithful qi economy (functional but free).
 11638→  6. **Altitude computation uses Heightmap.WORLD_SURFACE which may include trees.** A cultivator flying over a forest will cruise at treetop+4 blocks, which is high. Over a plains biome, they cruise at surface+4. The visual is inconsistent. A future CRON should use Heightmap.WORLD_SURFACE but subtract tree height, or use Heightmap.OCEAN_FLOOR (which ignores leaves) for more consistent cruise altitude. Score 7/10 for altitude handling (works, but visually inconsistent across biomes).
 11639→  7. **The flight goal does not check for ceiling/overhang collisions.** If a cultivator flies under a mountain overhang (e.g., into a cave entrance while flying), they will clip through the overhang. A future CRON should ray-cast upward and clamp the cruise altitude below any ceiling. Score 5/10 for ceiling handling (no clipping checks).
 11640→  8. **The Foundation+ realm gate is keyword-based, not enum-based.** isFoundationOrHigher() uses string-contains checks ("foundation", "core", "soul", etc.) rather than comparing against the RealmId enum ordinal. This is robust to new realm strings being added but is also fragile (a realm named "Soul Refining Sect" would incorrectly pass the "soul" check, even though Soul Refining is a sect name not a cultivation realm). Score 7/10 for robustness (works for current data, but should ideally parse to RealmId and compare ordinals).
