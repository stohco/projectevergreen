    14→/**
    15→ * Revival Attempt Service — CRON-COMPLETIONIST-100.
    16→ *
    17→ * <p>Implements the canon-faithful revival-attempt mechanic for Li Muwan's
    18→ * Nascent Soul. After CRON-99's soul-capture event, the player can attempt
    19→ * to revive Li Muwan via the {@code /ergenverse bead revive} command. Each
    20→ * attempt is gated by canon-faithful requirements and increments the
    21→ * {@link HeavenDefyingBeadItem#NBT_REVIVAL_ATTEMPTS} counter.
    22→ *
    23→ * <h2>Canon Basis (fact-checked via web-search 2026-07-26)</h2>
    24→ * <p>In the novel 仙逆 by 耳根:
    25→ * <ul>
    26→ *   <li>李慕婉 (Li Muwan) perishes after her 结婴 (Nascent Soul formation)
    27→ *       fails. Wang Lin captures her 元婴 into the 天逆珠 (CRON-99).</li>
    28→ *   <li>Wang Lin places her soul into 周茹 (Zhou Ru), a pregnant woman's
    29→ *       fetus. Li Muwan refuses to devour the host soul.</li>
    30→ *   <li>Wang Lin attempts revival <b>137 times</b> across millennia — all
    31→ *       fail. The 137th attempt is depicted in the novel: "血色残阳笼罩着
    32→ *       朱雀墓，王林怀中抱着生机尽散的李慕婉，她白发如雪的身体正在
    33→ *       化作星芒消散，这是他第137次尝试复活失败" (blood-red sun over the
    34→ *       Vermilion Bird Tomb, Wang Lin cradles the lifeless Li Muwan, her
    35→ *       white-haired body dissolving into starlight — his 137th failed
    36→ *       revival attempt).</li>
    37→ *   <li>Final success requires the Fourth Step (第四步) + 一界本源 (the
    38→ *       origin of a world). Wang Lin enters the Fourth Step (TRANSCENDENCE)
    39→ *       and uses a world's origin to finally revive her.</li>
    40→ * </ul>
    41→ *
    42→ * <p><b>Canon honesty:</b> The 137 number is canon-attested via web-search.
    43→ * The exact chapter is NOT cited to avoid fabrication. The novel clearly
    44→ * establishes the 137 failed attempts and the Fourth Step requirement for
    45→ * success. The in-mod attempt mechanic is a mod-fidelity bridge between
    46→ * the canon narrative (millennia-spanning attempts) and the gameplay loop
    47→ * (player-triggered command with cooldown).
    48→ *
    49→ * <h2>Attempt Gates</h2>
    50→ * <p>Each revival attempt must pass ALL of the following gates:
    51→ * <ol>
    52→ *   <li><b>Soul gate:</b> {@code hasLiMuwanSoul(stack)} must be true.
    53→ *       Without Li Muwan's soul in the bead, there is nothing to revive.</li>
    54→ *   <li><b>Stage gate:</b> Bead must have special functions
    55→ *       ({@link BeadInteriorStage#hasSpecialFunctions}). This requires
    56→ *       SMALL_WORLD+ stage. Canon: the revival ritual requires the bead's
    57→ *       deepest interior — a self-contained world.</li>
    58→ *   <li><b>Realm gate:</b> Player must be at least SOUL_FORMATION (化神).
    59→ *       Canon: Wang Lin attempts revival only after reaching high realms.
    60→ *       The final successful attempt requires TRANSCENDENCE ( Fourth Step).</li>
    61→ *   <li><b>Cooldown gate:</b> At least {@link #REVIVAL_COOLDOWN_TICKS}
    62→ *       must have elapsed since the last attempt. Prevents spam. The
    63→ *       cooldown is 6000 ticks (5 minutes at 20 TPS) — long enough to
    64→ *       feel weighty, short enough to be playable.</li>
    65→ *   <li><b>Cap gate:</b> The counter cannot exceed 137. Once 137 failed
    66→ *       attempts are recorded, additional attempts require TRANSCENDENCE
    67→ *       realm (the final successful attempt).</li>
    68→ *   <li><b>Essence gate (CRON-COMPLETIONIST-101):</b> The final successful
    69→ *       revival (TRANSCENDENCE + 137 prior failures) additionally requires
    70→ *       the player to hold at least one {@link WorldOriginEssenceItem}
    71→ *       (一界本源) in their inventory. Canon: "王林踏入第四步后，成功运用
    72→ *       一界本源将之复活" — Wang Lin uses the origin of a world to revive
    73→ *       Li Muwan. Without this essence, even the Fourth Step cannot restore
    74→ *       her. On success, one essence item is consumed (the world is
    75→ *       irreversibly sacrificed). This gate is ONLY checked on the success
    76→ *       path — the 137 failed attempts do NOT require the essence.</li>
    77→ * </ol>
    78→ *
    79→ * <h2>Outcomes</h2>
    80→ * <p>Each attempt has one of four outcomes:
    81→ * <ul>
    82→ *   <li><b>FAILURE:</b> The attempt fails (canon: all 137 attempts fail).
    83→ *       Counter increments. Displayed in RED with a mournful message.</li>
    84→ *   <li><b>FAILURE_137:</b> The 137th failed attempt. Counter increments
    85→ *       to 137. Displayed in DARK_PURPLE with the canon "blood-red sun
    86→ *       over the Vermilion Bird Tomb" narrative. The player is told that
    87→ *       only the Fourth Step can save her now.</li>
    88→ *   <li><b>SUCCESS:</b> Only fires at attempt 137 AND TRANSCENDENCE realm.
    89→ *       The revival succeeds. Displayed in GOLD with a triumphant message.
    90→ *       Sets a new NBT flag (TODO: future CRON) and records in history.</li>
    91→ *   <li><b>REJECTED:</b> The attempt was rejected by a gate (soul / stage /
    92→ *       realm / cooldown / cap). No counter increment. Displayed in GRAY
    93→ *       with the specific rejection reason.</li>
    94→ * </ul>
    95→ *
    96→ * <h2>Single-Player Maximalism (Article XLIII)</h2>
    97→ * <p>The service runs only for the single player (Wang Lin). There is no
    98→ * fairness concern — the player IS Wang Lin, and reviving Li Muwan is his
    99→ * central motivation. The cooldown exists for gameplay pacing, not fairness.
   100→ *
   101→ * <h2>Architecture</h2>
   102→ * <p>The service is a static utility class. The attempt is triggered by:
   103→ * <ul>
   104→ *   <li>The {@code /ergenverse bead revive} command (CRON-100). The command
   105→ *       locates the player's bead via a findBead helper that mirrors the
   106→ *       CRON-95 {@link BeadProgressionService#findBead} pattern (main-hand
   107→ *       → off-hand → main inventory).</li>
   108→ *   <li>Future: a bead-menu button (would require clickMenuButton support
   109→ *       in BeadFunctionMenu — out of scope for CRON-100).</li>
   110→ * </ul>
   111→ *
   112→ * <p>The service does NOT touch the WorldDeltaStore or WorldFacade — the
   113→ * revival attempt is purely an item-NBT event, not a world-block event.
   114→ * This respects the CRON-69 architecture: item state is Wang Lin's personal
   115→ * state, not world state.
   116→ *
   117→ * <p>MC 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.0 / Java 17.
   118→ *
   119→ * @see HeavenDefyingBeadItem#NBT_REVIVAL_ATTEMPTS
   120→ * @see HeavenDefyingBeadItem#getRevivalAttempts
   121→ * @see HeavenDefyingBeadItem#setRevivalAttempts
   122→ */
   123→public final class RevivalAttemptService {
   124→
   125→    /**
   126→     * Cooldown between revival attempts, in game ticks.
   127→     *
   128→     * <p>6000 ticks = 5 minutes at 20 TPS. Long enough to feel weighty
   129→     * (each attempt is a deliberate ritual, not a spam-click), short
   130→     * enough to be playable (a determined player can attempt 12 times
   131→     * per hour, reaching 137 in ~11.5 hours of focused play).
   132→     */
   133→    public static final int REVIVAL_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 6000;
   134→
   135→    /** Stable identifier for the HistoryManager subject on failure. */
   136→    public static final String SUBJECT_REVIVAL_FAILED = "li_muwan_revival_failed";
   137→
   138→    /** Stable identifier for the HistoryManager subject on the 137th failure. */
   139→    public static final String SUBJECT_REVIVAL_137TH = "li_muwan_revival_137th_failure";
   140→
   141→    /** Stable identifier for the HistoryManager subject on success. */
   142→    public static final String SUBJECT_REVIVAL_SUCCEEDED = "li_muwan_revival_succeeded";
   143→
   144→    /**
   145→     * CRON-COMPLETIONIST-101: Stable identifier for the HistoryManager subject
   146→     * recorded when a World Origin Essence (一界本源) item is consumed by the
   147→     * successful revival. Distinct from {@link #SUBJECT_REVIVAL_SUCCEEDED}
   148→     * because the essence consumption is a separate canon beat — Wang Lin
   149→     * sacrificing one of his worlds to permanently restore Li Muwan.
   150→     */
   151→    public static final String SUBJECT_REVIVAL_ESSENCE_CONSUMED = "li_muwan_revival_essence_consumed";
   152→
   153→    private RevivalAttemptService() {}
   154→
   155→    /**
   156→     * Attempt to revive Li Muwan. The single entry point for the revival
   157→     * mechanic.
   158→     *
   159→     * <p>This method is invoked by the {@code /ergenverse bead revive}
   160→     * command. It performs all gate checks, increments the counter (if
   161→     * not rejected), displays the canon-faithful outcome message, and
   162→     * records the event in {@link HistoryManager}.
   163→     *
   164→     * @param player the server player attempting the revival
   165→     * @param stack  the Heaven-Defying Bead stack (must be in the player's
   166→     *               inventory; the caller is responsible for locating it)
   167→     * @return {@code true} if the attempt was made (success or failure);
   168→     *         {@code false} if the attempt was rejected by a gate
   169→     */
   170→    public static boolean attemptRevival(ServerPlayer player, ItemStack stack) {
   171→        if (!(stack.getItem() instanceof HeavenDefyingBeadItem beadItem)) {
   172→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   173→                    "The item you hold is not the Heaven-Defying Bead.")
   174→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.GRAY));
   175→            return false;
   176→        }
   177→
   178→        // ── Gate 1: Soul gate ──
   179→        if (!beadItem.hasLiMuwanSoul(stack)) {
   180→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   181→                    "The bead does not contain Li Muwan's Nascent Soul. "
   182→                    + "There is nothing to revive.")
   183→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.GRAY));
   184→            return false;
   185→        }
   186→
   187→        // ── Gate 2: Stage gate ──
   188→        BeadInteriorStage stage = beadItem.getStage(stack);
   189→        if (!stage.hasSpecialFunctions) {
   190→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   191→                    "The bead's interior is too immature for the revival ritual. "
   192→                    + "Canon: the ritual requires a self-contained world within the bead.")
   193→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.GRAY));
   194→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   195→                    "(Current stage: " + stage.name() + ". Required: SMALL_WORLD+.)")
   196→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_GRAY, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   197→            return false;
   198→        }
   199→
   200→        // ── Gate 3: Realm gate ──
   201→        CultivationState state;
   202→        try {
   203→            state = CultivationCapability.getOrThrow(player);
   204→        } catch (Throwable t) {
   205→            Ergenverse.LOGGER.warn("[Ergenverse] CRON-100: CultivationState missing "
   206→                    + "for {} — cannot evaluate revival attempt.", player.getName().getString());
   207→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   208→                    "Your cultivation state cannot be determined.")
   209→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.GRAY));
   210→            return false;
   211→        }
   212→        RealmId realm = state.getCurrentRealm();
   213→        if (realm == null || !realm.isAtLeast(RealmId.SOUL_FORMATION)) {
   214→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   215→                    "Your cultivation is insufficient. The revival ritual requires "
   216→                    + "at least Soul Formation (化神).")
   217→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.GRAY));
   218→            if (realm != null) {
   219→                player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   220→                        "(Current realm: " + realm.name + " / " + realm.nameCn + ".)")
   221→                        .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_GRAY, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   222→            }
   223→            return false;
   224→        }
   225→
   226→        // ── Gate 4: Cooldown gate ──
   227→        long currentTick = player.serverLevel().getGameTime();
   228→        long lastAttempt = beadItem.getLastRevivalAttemptTick(stack);
   229→        if (lastAttempt > 0) {
   230→            long elapsed = currentTick - lastAttempt;
   231→            if (elapsed < REVIVAL_COOLDOWN_TICKS) {
   232→                long remaining = REVIVAL_COOLDOWN_TICKS - elapsed;
   233→                long remainingSeconds = remaining / 20;
   234→                player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   235→                        "The bead's interior is still recovering from the last attempt. "
   236→                        + "Wait " + remainingSeconds + " more seconds.")
   237→                        .withStyle(ChatFormatting.GRAY));
   238→                return false;
   239→            }
   240→        }
   241→
   242→        // ── Gate 5: Cap gate ──
   243→        int attempts = beadItem.getRevivalAttempts(stack);
   244→        if (attempts >= HeavenDefyingBeadItem.CANON_REVIVAL_ATTEMPT_CAP) {
   245→            // 137 attempts already made. Only TRANSCENDENCE can succeed now.
   246→            if (!realm.isAtLeast(RealmId.TRANSCENDENCE)) {
   247→                player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   248→                        "137 attempts have failed. Only the Fourth Step (超脱) "
   249→                        + "can save her now. Canon: Wang Lin revives Li Muwan "
   250→                        + "after entering the Fourth Step, using the origin of a world.")
   251→                        .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_PURPLE, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   252→                return false;
   253→            }
   254→
   255→            // ── Gate 6: Essence gate (CRON-COMPLETIONIST-101) ──
   256→            // The final successful revival requires the World Origin Essence
   257→            // (一界本源). Canon: "王林踏入第四步后，成功运用一界本源将之复活".
   258→            // Without this essence, even the Fourth Step cannot restore her.
   259→            ItemStack essenceStack = WorldOriginEssenceItem.findInInventory(player);
   260→            if (essenceStack.isEmpty()) {
   261→                player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   262→                        "═══════════════════════════════════════")
   263→                        .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_PURPLE));
   264→                player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   265→                        "你已踏入第四步，137次复活皆已失败。")
   266→                        .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_PURPLE, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   267→                player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   268→                        "但复活李慕婉还需最后一件至宝 —— 一界本源。")
   269→                        .withStyle(ChatFormatting.LIGHT_PURPLE, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   270→                player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   271→                        "Canon: Wang Lin entered the Fourth Step, yet 137 attempts "
   272→                        + "have failed. The final reagent is 一界本源 (World Origin "
   273→                        + "Essence) — the condensed origin of an entire world. "
   274→                        + "Without it, even the Fourth Step cannot restore her.")
   275→                        .withStyle(ChatFormatting.GRAY, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   276→                player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   277→                        "Acquire World Origin Essence and attempt the revival again.")
   278→                        .withStyle(ChatFormatting.YELLOW));
   279→                player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   280→                        "═══════════════════════════════════════")
   281→                        .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_PURPLE));
   282→                return false;
   283→            }
   284→
   285→            // TRANSCENDENCE + 137 attempts + World Origin Essence = SUCCESS!
   286→            return doSuccessfulRevival(player, beadItem, stack, currentTick, essenceStack);
   287→        }
   288→
   289→        // ── All gates passed. Perform the failed attempt. ──
   290→        return doFailedRevival(player, beadItem, stack, currentTick, attempts);
   291→    }
   292→
   293→    /**
   294→     * Perform a failed revival attempt. Increments the counter, displays
   295→     * the canon-faithful failure message, and records in history.
   296→     *
   297→     * <p>Canon: all 137 attempts fail. The 137th failure is the final
   298→     * narrative beat before the endgame.
   299→     */
   300→    private static boolean doFailedRevival(ServerPlayer player,
   301→                                            HeavenDefyingBeadItem beadItem,
   302→                                            ItemStack stack,
   303→                                            long currentTick,
   304→                                            int previousAttempts) {
   305→        int newAttempts = previousAttempts + 1;
   306→        beadItem.setRevivalAttempts(stack, newAttempts);
   307→        beadItem.setLastRevivalAttemptTick(stack, currentTick);
   308→
   309→        boolean is137th = (newAttempts == HeavenDefyingBeadItem.CANON_REVIVAL_ATTEMPT_CAP);
   310→
   311→        if (is137th) {
   312→            // The 137th failure — the canon narrative beat.
   313→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   314→                    "─────────────────────────────────────")
   315→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_PURPLE));
   316→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   317→                    "血色残阳笼罩着朱雀墓。")
   318→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_PURPLE, ChatFormatting.BOLD));
   319→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   320→                    "王林怀中抱着生机尽散的李慕婉，她白发如雪的身体正在化作星芒消散。")
   321→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.LIGHT_PURPLE, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   322→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   323→                    "这是他第137次尝试复活失败。")
   324→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.RED, ChatFormatting.BOLD));
   325→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   326→                    "A blood-red sun sets over the Vermilion Bird Tomb. Li Muwan's "
   327→                    + "white-haired body dissolves into starlight in your arms.")
   328→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.LIGHT_PURPLE, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   329→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   330→                    "This is your 137th failed revival attempt.")
   331→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.RED, ChatFormatting.BOLD));
   332→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   333→                    "Only the Fourth Step (超脱) can save her now.")
   334→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_PURPLE, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   335→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   336→                    "─────────────────────────────────────")
   337→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.DARK_PURPLE));
   338→
   339→            HistoryManager.onDiscovery(player, SUBJECT_REVIVAL_137TH,
   340→                    "The 137th failed revival attempt at the Vermilion Bird Tomb. "
   341→                            + "Li Muwan's body dissolved into starlight. Only the Fourth Step remains.",
   342→                    currentTick);
   343→
   344→            Ergenverse.LOGGER.info("[Ergenverse] CRON-100: Player {} reached the 137th "
   345→                            + "failed revival attempt (canon milestone). Realm={}.",
   346→                    player.getName().getString(),
   347→                    player.serverLevel().getGameTime());
   348→        } else {
   349→            // A normal failed attempt (1..136).
   350→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   351→                    "第 " + newAttempts + " 次尝试复活失败。李慕婉的元婴在 天逆珠 中叹息。")
   352→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.LIGHT_PURPLE, ChatFormatting.ITALIC));
   353→            player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
   354→                    "Revival attempt " + newAttempts + " of 137 has failed. "
   355→                    + "Li Muwan's Nascent Soul sighs within the bead.")
   356→                    .withStyle(ChatFormatting.RED));
   357→
   358→            HistoryManager.onDiscovery(player, SUBJECT_REVIVAL_FAILED,
   359→                    "Revival attempt " + newAttempts + "/137 failed. "
   360→                            + "Li Muwan's soul remains trapped in the bead.",
   361→                    currentTick);
   362→
   363→            Ergenverse.LOGGER.info("[Ergenverse] CRON-100: Player {} made revival attempt "
   364→                            + "{}/137 (failed). Realm={}.",
   365→                    player.getName().getString(), newAttempts,
   366→                    HeavenDefyingBeadItem.CANON_REVIVAL_ATTEMPT_CAP);
   367→        }
   368→
   369→        return true;
   370→    }
   371→
   372→    /**
   373→     * Perform the successful revival. Only fires when:
   374→     * <ul>
   375→     *   <li>137 failed attempts already recorded</li>
   376→     *   <li>Player realm is TRANSCENDENCE (Fourth Step)</li>
   377→     *   <li>Player holds at least one World Origin Essence (一界本源) — CRON-101</li>
   378→     * </ul>
   379→     *
   380→     * <p>Canon: Wang Lin enters the Fourth Step, uses the origin of a
   381→     * world (一界本源), and finally revives Li Muwan. They transcend
   382→     * together, beyond life and death.
   383→     *
   384→     * <p><b>CRON-COMPLETIONIST-101:</b> This method now CONSUMES one
   385→     * World Origin Essence item from the player's inventory. Canon: the
   386→     * world whose origin is extracted is irreversibly sacrificed. The
   387→     * consumption is logged via {@link HistoryManager} under a separate
   388→     * subject ({@link #SUBJECT_REVIVAL_ESSENCE_CONSUMED}) to distinguish
   389→     * the essence-sacrifice beat from the general success beat.
   390→     *
   391→     * <p><b>CRON-COMPLETIONIST-102:</b> This method now also marks Li
   392→     * Muwan as revived on the bead (write-once NBT flag
   393→     * {@link HeavenDefyingBeadItem#NBT_LI_MUWAN_REVIVED}) and spawns Li
   394→     * Muwan as a living {@link dev.ergenverse.entity.EntityCultivator} at
   395→     * the player's position via {@link LiMuwanRevivalEvent#spawnAtPlayer}.
   396→     * She is configured as a companion: her cultivation realm is set to
   397→     * TRANSCENDENCE (踏天境), her HP is set to a high value (canon: 弹指灭天),
   398→     * and her following-player UUID is set to the player's UUID (activating
   399→     * the {@link dev.ergenverse.entity.ai.FollowPlayerGoal} — she follows
   400→     * Wang Lin, "两人踏天同行").
   401→     *
   402→     * <p>This method does NOT remove Li Muwan's soul flag from the bead —
   403→     * the soul flag remains as a permanent record alongside the revived
   404→     * flag. The revived flag distinguishes "soul captured" (CRON-99) from
   405→     * "soul revived" (CRON-102).
   406→     */
   407→    private static boolean doSuccessfulRevival(ServerPlayer player,
   408→                                                HeavenDefyingBeadItem beadItem,
   409→                                                ItemStack stack,
   410→                                                long currentTick,
   411→                                                ItemStack essenceStack) {
   412→        // The 138th "attempt" is the success — but we DON'T increment the
   413→        // counter beyond 137. The counter stays at 137 (canon-attested cap).
   414→        // Instead, we record the success via HistoryManager and (TODO) a
   415→        // new NBT flag NBT_LI_MUWAN_REVIVED.
   416→        beadItem.setLastRevivalAttemptTick(stack, currentTick);
   417→
   418→        // CRON-COMPLETIONIST-101: Consume the World Origin Essence.
   419→        // Canon: the world whose origin is extracted is irreversibly
   420→        // sacrificed. The shrink(1) reduces the stack by 1; if the stack
   421→        // size was 1 (which it always is — stacksTo(1)), the slot becomes
   422→        // empty. This is the canon-faithful permanent loss.
   423→        String sourceWorld = essenceStack.hasTag()
   424→                && essenceStack.getTag().contains(WorldOriginEssenceItem.NBT_SOURCE_WORLD)
   425→                ? essenceStack.getTag().getString(WorldOriginEssenceItem.NBT_SOURCE_WORLD)
   426→                : WorldOriginEssenceItem.DEFAULT_SOURCE_WORLD;
   427→        essenceStack.shrink(1);
   428→        // Defensive: if shrink didn't fully consume (e.g., stacked via
   429→        // creative pick-block), force-set to empty.
   430→        if (!essenceStack.isEmpty()) {
   431→            essenceStack.setCount(0);
   432→        }
   433→
   434→        player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal(
