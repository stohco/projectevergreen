 13616→- HARSH SELF-CRITIQUE (hyper-analytical):
 13617→  1. **Greedy reduction is 3x for interior chunks, not 5-10x.** The 5-10x
 13618→     target is hit ONLY for chunks with exposed borders (no neighbors =
 13619→     11.3x) because the massive underground stone walls merge into single
 13620→     quads. For interior chunks (neighbors loaded, borders culled), the
 13621→     remaining faces are the terrain SURFACE — a staircase of grass/dirt at
 13622→     varying heights that can't merge across different y-levels. 3x is the
 13623→     honest steady-state number for natural varied terrain. Score 6/10 vs
 13624→     the stated target. To hit 5x steady-state I'd need height-correlated
 13625→     meshing or a second pass that merges vertical strips of equal-height
 13626→     columns. NOT DONE.
 13627→  2. **No per-vertex AO.** The shader uses face-direction ambient (top/side/
 13628→     bottom) — this is "basic AO" in name only. True 4-corner vertex AO
 13629→     with greedy-meshing (requires AO-consistency for merge) is the standard
 13630→     AAA approach but complicates the merge key. The current lighting looks
 13631→     flat on large walls. Score 4/10 for AO. Contact shadows at block edges
 13632→     are completely absent.
 13633→  3. **Water is opaque.** No translucency pass — the shader uses alphaTest
 13634→     0.5 + discard, so water renders as a solid blue surface. No depth-
 13635→     write-blended transparent material, no refraction, no underwater fog.
 13636→     Rivers look like painted floors, not water. Score 3/10 for water.
 13637→  4. **No BatchedMesh.** Each chunk is its own Mesh → its own draw call.
 13638→     renderDistance=8 → ~289 draw calls. Three's frustum culling helps, but
 13639→     the draw-call overhead is real. Three r185's BatchedMesh would merge
 13640→     all chunks into 1 draw call — NOT IMPLEMENTED. Score 5/10 for GPU
 13641→     throughput. 60fps is achievable on desktop but marginal on mobile.
 13642→  5. **No texture mipmaps.** NearestFilter + no mipmaps = aliasing on
 13643→     distant chunks. The fog hides the worst of it, but distant texture
 13644→     edges shimmer. A texture-array (DataArrayTexture) with per-tile
 13645→     mipmaps would fix this — NOT DONE. Score 5/10 for distant visuals.
 13646→  6. **Mesher is single-threaded.** 44ms/chunk on the main thread. A 289-
 13647→     chunk initial load = ~13 seconds of meshing, hitching the main thread.
 13648→     A Web Worker meshing pipeline (chunk → worker → geometry → main) is
 13649→     the AAA solution. NOT DONE. Score 4/10 for load-time UX. The
 13650→     chunksPerFrame=2 amortization prevents per-frame hitches but the
 13651→     initial pop-in is slow.
