    30→/**
    31→ * BlueprintChunkGenerator — a true algorithmically-independent chunk generator
    32→ * for Planet Suzaku whose surface terrain is derived from the
    33→ * {@link PlanetSuzakuBlueprint}'s canon geography (plus a tiny deterministic
    34→ * value-noise perturbation seeded by {@link DeterministicSeedHandler#CANON_SEED}),
    35→ * <em>not</em> from {@code minecraft:overworld} noise.
    36→ *
    37→ * <h2>CRON-COMPLETIONIST-60 — TRUE ALGORITHMIC INDEPENDENCE (point 10)</h2>
    38→ *
    39→ * <p>The CRON-71 design (this class's prior form) only wrapped
    40→ * {@link NoiseBasedChunkGenerator} and overrode {@link #getBaseHeight} to add
    41→ * canon terrain offsets on top of vanilla noise. The surface shape was still
    42→ * determined by {@code minecraft:overworld} noise — only nudged a few blocks
    43→ * per canon location. That was a "noise with offset" generator, not a "blueprint"
    44→ * generator. It failed the architectural goal of point 10: <i>"Terrain columns
    45→ * come from the blueprint+layers, not minecraft:overworld noise."</i>
    46→ *
    47→ * <p>This version (CRON-COMPLETIONIST-60) closes that gap:
    48→ * <ul>
    49→ *   <li>{@link #fillFromNoise} is overridden to fill each column from
    50→ *       {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)}, a pure deterministic function
    51→ *       of the blueprint's canon locations + {@link DeterministicSeedHandler#CANON_SEED}.</li>
    52→ *   <li>{@link #getBaseHeight} returns {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)}.</li>
    53→ *   <li>{@link #getBaseColumn} returns a column built from
    54→ *       {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)} — bedrock at the bottom, stone
    55→ *       up to the canon surface, water up to sea level, air above.</li>
    56→ * </ul>
    57→ *
    58→ * <p>The wrapped {@link NoiseBasedChunkGenerator} is still used for
    59→ * {@link #applyCarvers} (caves/ravines) and {@link #buildSurface} (grass/sand/
    60→ * snow/etc. biome surface rules). Those methods use vanilla's noise-derived
    61→ * carving and surface data, which is independent of the surface heightmap —
    62→ * they carve <i>through</i> whatever stone we placed, and apply surface rules
    63→ * on top of whatever stone surface we placed. This preserves caves, ores
    64→ * (ores are placed by feature decoration which queries existing stone), and
    65→ * biome surface variation for free.
    66→ *
    67→ * <p><b>Canon-driven surface formula:</b>
    68→ * <pre>{@code
    69→ *   canonSurfaceHeight(x, z) =
    70→ *       BASE_HEIGHT (64, one above sea level)
    71→ *     + sum_over_nearby_canon_locations(getTerrainWarpForLocation(loc)
    72→ *                                        * linearDecayFactor(distance, RADIUS=200))
    73→ *     + canonNoiseVariation(x, z)   // bilinear value noise, 8-block period,
    74→ *                                    // amplitude ±8, seeded by CANON_SEED
    75→ * }</pre>
    76→ *
    77→ * <p>The result: Heng Yue Mountain is always raised +30 near (4200, -1400),
    78→ * the Sea of Devils is always lowered −30 near (6000, -1184), Wang Family
    79→ * Village sits at +8 above sea level on a hill, etc. — every playthrough,
    80→ * regardless of vanilla noise seed. The hand-placed spawn at (3842, -1184)
    81→ * always lands on the same hill. This is <i>true algorithmic independence</i>
    82→ * from {@code minecraft:overworld} noise.
    83→ *
    84→ * <p><b>Codec &amp; registration:</b> Registered as {@code ergenverse:blueprint}
    85→ * via {@link ErgenverseChunkGenerators#BLUEPRINT}. The dimension JSON at
    86→ * {@code data/ergenverse/dimension/planet_suzaku.json} references the generator
    87→ * type as {@code ergenverse:blueprint} with {@code settings: minecraft:overworld}
    88→ * (the overworld settings are used for cave/surface-rule configuration, NOT for
    89→ * surface terrain shape — that comes from this generator).
    90→ *
    91→ * <p><b>Canon fidelity (fact-checked against 仙逆):</b>
    92→ * <ul>
    93→ *   <li>恒岳山 (Heng Yue Mountain): Zhao Country's largest mountain, raised +30.</li>
    94→ *   <li>修魔海 (Sea of Devils): vast perilous sea east of Zhao, lowered −30 → ocean.</li>
    95→ *   <li>朱雀墓 (Suzaku Tomb): underground inheritance site, surface depressed −12.</li>
    96→ *   <li>Wang Family Village: "赵国某偏僻小山村" — remote mountain village, raised +8.</li>
    97→ *   <li>藤家城 (Teng Family City): powerful family city in Zhao, raised +10.</li>
    98→ *   <li>雪域国 (Snow Domain): cold elevated country, raised +10.</li>
    99→ *   <li>恒岳派 / 炼魂宗 / 玄道宗 / 洛河门: mountain sects, raised +12 to +15.</li>
   100→ * </ul>
   101→ *
   102→ * <p><b>Known trade-offs (hyper-analytical self-critique):</b>
   103→ * <ul>
   104→ *   <li>The base noise variation is bilinear value noise (8-block period,
   105→ *       ±8 amplitude). This is intentionally crude — its purpose is to break
   106→ *       the perfectly-flat plateau that pure canon warping would produce
   107→ *       between canon locations, NOT to mimic natural terrain. Real terrain
   108→ *       variety will come from biome surface rules (grass/sand/snow) applied
   109→ *       by {@link #buildSurface}, not from terrain noise.</li>
   110→ *   <li>Caves are carved by vanilla carvers, which use vanilla noise to
   111→ *       determine WHERE to carve. Because our surface is canon-shaped (not
   112→ *       vanilla-noise-shaped), caves may occasionally carve into air (above
   113→ *       our surface where vanilla expected stone) or fail to carve (below our
   114→ *       surface where vanilla expected air). The net effect: caves still
   115→ *       exist but their density/surface-entrance distribution may differ
   116→ *       from a vanilla world. This is acceptable — the canon world has caves,
   117→ *       they just don't align with vanilla noise.</li>
   118→ *   <li>Ores are placed by feature decoration, which queries existing stone
   119→ *       blocks. Since we fill stone up to the canon surface, ores will be
   120→ *       placed normally. No loss here.</li>
   121→ *   <li>Aquifers (underground water) are part of vanilla noise generation.
   122→ *       Skipping vanilla {@code fillFromNoise} means we lose aquifers. This
   123→ *       is a known limitation; aquifers are mostly invisible to the player
   124→ *       (underground water pools) and the Sea of Devils already provides
   125→ *       surface water via our canon fill.</li>
   126→ *   <li>The wrapped generator's {@link NoiseBasedChunkGenerator#fillFromNoise}
   127→ *       is NOT called, so {@code chunk.getOrCreateNoiseChunk(...)} will be
   128→ *       lazily computed on first access (by {@code applyCarvers} or
   129→ *       {@code buildSurface}). This is fine — vanilla supports lazy
   130→ *       NoiseChunk computation.</li>
   131→ * </ul>
   132→ *
   133→ * <p>MC 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.0 / Java 17.</p>
   134→ */

   226→    /**
   227→     * Fill the chunk with canon-derived terrain.
   228→     *
   229→     * <p>This is the heart of the algorithmic independence: surface height
   230→     * per column comes from {@link #canonSurfaceHeight(int, int)}, NOT from
   231→     * vanilla noise. The wrapped {@link NoiseBasedChunkGenerator#fillFromNoise}
   232→     * is NOT called — we generate terrain entirely from canon geography.
   233→     *
   234→     * <p>Vanilla {@link #applyCarvers} and {@link #buildSurface} will lazily
   235→     * compute the {@code NoiseChunk} they need for cave placement and surface
   236→     * rules; those operations do NOT depend on the surface heightmap that
   237→     * {@code fillFromNoise} would have produced.
   238→     */
   239→    @Override
   240→    public CompletableFuture<ChunkAccess> fillFromNoise(Executor executor, Blender blender,
   241→                                                           RandomState randomState,
   242→                                                           StructureManager structureManager,
   243→                                                           ChunkAccess chunk) {
   244→        int minY = chunk.getMinBuildHeight();
   245→        int maxY = chunk.getMaxBuildHeight();
   246→        int minChunkX = chunk.getPos().getMinBlockX();
   247→        int minChunkZ = chunk.getPos().getMinBlockZ();
   248→
   249→        // Cache block states — BlockState lookup is cheap but de-virtualized
   250→        // local refs are still marginally faster in a hot loop.
   251→        final BlockState stone = Blocks.STONE.defaultBlockState();
   252→        final BlockState water = Blocks.WATER.defaultBlockState();
   253→        final BlockState bedrock = Blocks.BEDROCK.defaultBlockState();
   254→
   255→        // MutableBlockPos avoids per-block allocation. setBlockState on a
   256→        // ProtoChunk during chunk-gen does NOT trigger lighting/scheduled
   257→        // tick updates — it just writes the section state.
   258→        BlockPos.MutableBlockPos pos = new BlockPos.MutableBlockPos();
   259→
   260→        for (int localX = 0; localX < 16; localX++) {
   261→            for (int localZ = 0; localZ < 16; localZ++) {
   262→                int worldX = minChunkX + localX;
   263→                int worldZ = minChunkZ + localZ;
   264→                int surfaceHeight = canonSurfaceHeight(worldX, worldZ);
   265→
   266→                for (int y = minY; y < maxY; y++) {
   267→                    BlockState state;
   268→                    if (y == minY) {
   269→                        state = bedrock;
   270→                    } else if (y < surfaceHeight) {
   271→                        state = stone;
   272→                    } else if (y < SEA_LEVEL) {
   273→                        state = water;
   274→                    } else {
   275→                        // Air — leave the section's default (air). Skip
   276→                        // setBlockState to avoid 65K useless writes per
   277→                        // column above the surface.
   278→                        continue;
   279→                    }
   280→
   281→                    pos.set(worldX, y, worldZ);
   282→                    chunk.setBlockState(pos, state, false);
   283→                }
   284→            }
   285→        }
   286→
   287→        // Synchronous fill — return already-completed future. Vanilla's
   288→        // NoiseBasedChunkGenerator runs fillFromNoise on a worker thread via
   289→        // supplyAsync(executor); we don't need to because our fill is
   290→        // CPU-bound and fast (no noise sampling, no aquifer computation).
   291→        return CompletableFuture.completedFuture(chunk);
   292→    }

   360→    /**
   361→     * Get the base noise column at (x, z), built from canon surface height.
   362→     * Returns bedrock at the bottom, stone up to the canon surface, water
   363→     * up to sea level, air above.
   364→     */
   365→    @Override
   366→    public NoiseColumn getBaseColumn(int x, int z, LevelHeightAccessor level,
   367→                                      RandomState randomState) {
   368→        int minY = level.getMinBuildHeight();
   369→        int height = level.getHeight();
   370→        int surfaceHeight = canonSurfaceHeight(x, z);
   371→
   372→        BlockState[] states = new BlockState[height];
   373→        for (int i = 0; i < height; i++) {
   374→            int y = minY + i;
   375→            if (y == minY) {
   376→                states[i] = Blocks.BEDROCK.defaultBlockState();
   377→            } else if (y < surfaceHeight) {
   378→                states[i] = Blocks.STONE.defaultBlockState();
   379→            } else if (y < SEA_LEVEL) {
   380→                states[i] = Blocks.WATER.defaultBlockState();
   381→            } else {
   382→                states[i] = Blocks.AIR.defaultBlockState();
   383→            }
   384→        }
   385→        return new NoiseColumn(minY, states);
   386→    }

   400→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   401→    //  CANON SURFACE HEIGHT — pure deterministic function of (x, z)
   402→    // ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   403→
   404→    /**
   405→     * Compute the canon-derived surface height at (worldX, worldZ).
   406→     *
   407→     * <p>{@code BASE_SURFACE_HEIGHT + canonTerrainOffset + canonNoiseVariation},
   408→     * clamped to a non-negative minimum (bedrock layer at {@code minY} must
   409→     * always have at least one stone block above it).
   410→     *
   411→     * <p><b>CRON-COMPLETIONIST-67:</b> this method is now {@code public} so that
   412→     * structure builders can resolve their center Y from the <i>same canon
   413→     * authority</i> that the chunk generator uses — eliminating the heightmap
   414→     * race condition where {@code level.getHeightmapPos(MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES, ...)}
   415→     * would return y=0 or a stale value if the chunk at the canon center wasn't
   416→     * loaded yet when {@code buildForChunk} fired for an adjacent chunk. The
   417→     * canon surface height is a pure deterministic function of (x, z) — it does
   418→     * NOT depend on chunk-load state, so it returns the correct Y every time,
   419→     * regardless of which chunks are loaded.
   420→     *
   421→     * <p>Builders should call this instead of {@code level.getHeightmapPos(...)}:
   422→     * <pre>{@code
   423→     *   int surfaceY = BlueprintChunkGenerator.canonSurfaceHeight(SECT_X, SECT_Z);
   424→     *   return new BlockPos(SECT_X, surfaceY, SECT_Z);
   425→     * }</pre>
   426→     */
   427→    public static int canonSurfaceHeight(int worldX, int worldZ) {
   428→        int offset = getCanonTerrainOffset(worldX, worldZ);
   429→        int noise = canonNoiseVariation(worldX, worldZ);
   430→        int h = BASE_SURFACE_HEIGHT + offset + noise;
   431→        // Clamp: never let the surface drop to or below 1 (need at least
   432→        // bedrock + stone). Also never exceed a sane cap to avoid Y=320
   433→        // pillars on canon warps (current max warp is +30, so the cap is
   434→        // mostly a defensive measure).
   435→        if (h < 2) return 2;
   436→        if (h > 256) return 256;
   437→        return h;
   438→    }
   439→
   440→    /**
   441→     * Compute the canon terrain offset at (worldX, worldZ).
   442→     * Returns the sum of all nearby canon location influences.
   443→     * Positive = raise terrain (mountain), Negative = lower terrain (sea/tomb).
   444→     *
   445→     * <p>Each canon location has an influence that decays linearly from its
   446→     * center to {@link #TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS} blocks away. The offset is
   447→     * deterministic and based on the blueprint's geography.
   448→     */
   449→    static int getCanonTerrainOffset(int worldX, int worldZ) {
   450→        PlanetSuzakuBlueprint blueprint = PlanetSuzakuBlueprint.canonical();
   451→        int totalOffset = 0;
   452→
   453→        for (PlanetSuzakuBlueprint.CanonLocation loc : blueprint.allLocations().values()) {
   454→            int dx = worldX - loc.x;
   455→            int dz = worldZ - loc.z;
   456→            int distSq = dx * dx + dz * dz;
   457→
   458→            if (distSq >= TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS_SQ) continue;
   459→
   460→            double dist = Math.sqrt(distSq);
   461→            double factor = 1.0 - (dist / TERRAIN_WARP_RADIUS);
   462→
   463→            int warp = getTerrainWarpForLocation(loc);
   464→            totalOffset += (int) (warp * factor);
   465→        }
   466→
   467→        return totalOffset;
   468→    }
   469→
   470→    /**
   471→     * Compute a small deterministic value-noise variation at (worldX, worldZ).
   472→     * Bilinear interpolation of a per-cell hash, scaled to ±{@link #NOISE_AMPLITUDE}.
   473→     * Period = {@link #NOISE_PERIOD} blocks.
   474→     *
   475→     * <p>The hash is seeded by {@link DeterministicSeedHandler#CANON_SEED},
   476→     * so the variation is identical every save. The purpose of this noise is
   477→     * NOT to mimic natural terrain — it is to break the perfectly-flat plateau
   478→     * that pure canon warping would produce between canon locations.
   479→     */
   480→    static int canonNoiseVariation(int worldX, int worldZ) {
   481→        int cellX = Math.floorDiv(worldX, NOISE_PERIOD);
   482→        int cellZ = Math.floorDiv(worldZ, NOISE_PERIOD);
   483→        int fracX = worldX - cellX * NOISE_PERIOD;
   484→        int fracZ = worldZ - cellZ * NOISE_PERIOD;
   485→
   486→        // Smoothstep interpolation weights
   487→        double sx = (double) fracX / NOISE_PERIOD;
   488→        double sz = (double) fracZ / NOISE_PERIOD;
   489→        sx = sx * sx * (3 - 2 * sx);
   490→        sz = sz * sz * (3 - 2 * sz);
   491→
   492→        // Four corner values in range [0, 2*NOISE_AMPLITUDE]
   493→        double v00 = noiseHash(cellX,     cellZ);
   494→        double v10 = noiseHash(cellX + 1, cellZ);
   495→        double v01 = noiseHash(cellX,     cellZ + 1);
   496→        double v11 = noiseHash(cellX + 1, cellZ + 1);
   497→
   498→        double ix0 = v00 + (v10 - v00) * sx;
   499→        double ix1 = v01 + (v11 - v01) * sx;
   500→        double val = ix0 + (ix1 - ix0) * sz;
   501→
   502→        // Center around zero: range [-NOISE_AMPLITUDE, +NOISE_AMPLITUDE]
   503→        return (int) Math.round(val - NOISE_AMPLITUDE);
   504→    }
   505→
   506→    /**
   507→     * Deterministic hash → value in [0, 2 * NOISE_AMPLITUDE]. Uses a
   508→     * splitmix64-style mixing of (cellX, cellZ, CANON_SEED) so that
   509→     * adjacent cells produce decorrelated values.
   510→     */
   511→    private static long noiseHash(int cellX, int cellZ) {
   512→        long h = DeterministicSeedHandler.CANON_SEED;
   513→        h ^= (long) cellX * 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15L;
   514→        h ^= (long) cellZ * 0xC2B2AE3D27D4EB4FL;
   515→        h ^= h >>> 33;
   516→        h *= 0xFF51AFD7ED558CCDL;
   517→        h ^= h >>> 33;
   518→        h *= 0xC4CEB9FE1A85EC53L;
   519→        h ^= h >>> 33;
   520→        // Map to [0, 2 * NOISE_AMPLITUDE]
   521→        return (h & 0xFFFFFFFFL) % (2L * NOISE_AMPLITUDE + 1);
   522→    }
   523→
   524→    /**
   525→     * Get the terrain warp height for a canon location.
   526→     * Positive = raise (mountains), Negative = lower (seas/tombs).
   527→     *
   528→     * <p><b>Canon fidelity (fact-checked against 仙逆):</b>
   529→     * <ul>
   530→     *   <li>恒岳山 (Heng Yue Mountain): the mountain where Heng Yue Sect is
   531→     *       located. Canon describes it as Zhao Country's largest mountain.
   532→     *       Raised terrain is correct.</li>

   534→     *       Lowered terrain (below sea level) creates ocean.</li>

   539→     *       Slight elevation fits the "mountain" description.</li>

   541→     *       Moderate elevation for a walled city.</li>
