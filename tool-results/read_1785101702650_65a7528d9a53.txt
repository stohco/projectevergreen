    29→/**
    30→ * Ergenverse — A Living Cultivation World.
    31→ *
    32→ * <p>This mod reconstructs the Er Gen cultivation multiverse inside Minecraft.
    33→ *
    34→ * <p><b>The Prime Directive ({@link WorldPhilosophy}):</b>
    35→ * <blockquote>
    36→ *   Never hide or reveal objects because of the player's level. Hide or
    37→ *   reveal interactions according to the laws of the world. The world is
    38→ *   objective and exists independently of the player. Cultivation increases
    39→ *   a character's ability to perceive, understand, and interact with deeper
    40→ *   layers of reality — it does not create or replace reality.
    41→ * </blockquote>
    42→ *
    43→ * <h2>Phase priority</h2>
    44→ * <ol>
    45→ *   <li><b>Phase 0 — The world exists before you arrive.</b> Living ecology,
    46→ *       atmosphere, danger, no cultivation, no UI, no Wang Lin.</li>
    47→ *   <li><b>Phase 1 — Discover cultivation.</b> Find a spirit vein, meditate,
    48→ *       break through. Perception shift: deer was always a Spirit Deer;
    49→ *       you just didn't know.</li>
    50→ *   <li><b>Phase 2 — Find a sect (ecosystem).</b> Outer → inner → core →
    51→ *       elders → patriarch, plus spirit beasts, herb fields, alchemy,
    52→ *       formations, nearby villages.</li>
    53→ *   <li><b>Phase 3 — World Pulse.</b> Every Minecraft day, the world
    54→ *       evolves independently of the player.</li>
    55→ *   <li><b>Phase 4 — Landmarks + Ocean dread.</b> Rare memorable landmarks.
    56→ *       Ocean depth system. World-scale entities whose presence reshapes
    57→ *       the environment.</li>
    58→ *   <li><b>Phase 5 — Perception as core mechanic.</b> Same entity,
    59→ *       different understanding at different tiers.</li>
    60→ *   <li><b>Phase 6 (delayed) — Wang Lin dimension.</b> Rumors →
    61→ *       investigation → eventual encounter.</li>
    62→ * </ol>
    63→ *
    64→ * <h2>Architecture</h2>
    65→ * <ul>
    66→ *   <li>{@link WorldPhilosophy} — the prime directive, encoded.</li>
    67→ *   <li>{@link dev.ergenverse.world.WorldLayer} — the three layers of reality
    68→ *       (Physical / Spiritual / Dao) that every chunk contains.</li>
    69→ *   <li>{@link WorldLaws} — every location knows why it exists; laws
    70→ *       determine what herbs grow, what beasts evolve, what techniques
    71→ *       work.</li>
    72→ *   <li>{@link dev.ergenverse.perception.Objective} — things that exist
    73→ *       independently of any observer.</li>
    74→ *   <li>{@link PerceptionEngine} — produces understanding of objective
    75→ *       reality. Does not change the world.</li>
    76→ *   <li>{@link dev.ergenverse.perception.DivineSense} — the transformative
    77→ *       sense mechanic.</li>
    78→ *   <li>{@link CausalEcology} — every spirit beast exists because of an
    79→ *       ecosystem. Exterminate herbivores → predators starve → herbs
    80→ *       spread → sects notice.</li>
    81→ *   <li>{@link CanonEngine} — canon confidence ratings; Level 0 forbidden
    82→ *       in known canon regions.</li>

   346→    /**
   347→     * Server tick — runs the three decoupled loops:
   348→     *
   349→     * <ol>
   350→     *   <li><b>CausalEcology</b> — every tick. The living ecology (spirit
   351→     *       beasts, herb growth, predator/prey). Existing system, not
   352→     *       touched.</li>
   353→     *   <li><b>WorldStateEngine</b> — every 24000 ticks (1 MC day). The
   354→     *       macro simulation: time events, migrations, karma resolution.
   355→     *       Advances divergence counter on WorldRuntimeState.</li>
   356→     *   <li><b>ReificationScan</b> — every 100 ticks (5 sec). The proximity
   357→     *       scanner that materializes canon NPCs when a player approaches.
   358→     *       Decoupled from chunk events to avoid cascading generation.</li>
   359→     * </ol>
   360→     *
   361→     * <p>These are THREE SEPARATE LOOPS with different frequencies and
   362→     * concerns. The prior design conflated ReificationScan with
   363→     * WorldStateEngine under one 24000-tick interval, which would mean a
   364→     * player could stand next to Wang Tiangui's spawn point for 20 minutes
   365→     * before he appeared. Now they're independent.
   366→     */
   367→    @SubscribeEvent
   368→    public void onServerTick(TickEvent.ServerTickEvent event) {
   369→        if (event.phase != TickEvent.Phase.END) return;
   370→        net.minecraft.server.level.ServerLevel overworld = event.getServer().overworld();
   371→        long ticks = overworld.getGameTime();
   372→
   373→        // ── Bootstrap: register WorldEventBus subscribers + NpcSpawnRegistry on first tick ──
   374→        // The bus is cleared on world unload, so subscribers must be
   375→        // re-registered each session. We do it on tick 1 (before any
   376→        // events can fire) and guard with a flag to avoid re-registering.
   377→        if (ticks == 1) {
   378→            dev.ergenverse.simulation.event.WorldEventBus.subscribe(
   379→                    new dev.ergenverse.simulation.event.QiDisturbanceSubscriber());
   380→            dev.ergenverse.simulation.event.WorldEventBus.subscribe(
   381→                    new dev.ergenverse.simulation.event.BirdFlightSubscriber());
   382→            dev.ergenverse.simulation.event.WorldEventBus.subscribe(
   383→                    new dev.ergenverse.simulation.event.ActivityInterruptionSubscriber());
   384→            dev.ergenverse.simulation.event.WorldEventBus.subscribe(
   385→                    new dev.ergenverse.npc.memory.MemoryEventSubscriber());
