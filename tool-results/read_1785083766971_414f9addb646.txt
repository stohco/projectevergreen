    21→/**
    22→ * Zhou Ru Kunxu Departure Event — CRON-COMPLETIONIST-112.
    23→ *
    24→ * <p>Implements the canon-faithful post-transfer quest step: after CRON-110's
    25→ * soul transfer (Li Muwan's 元婴 placed into 周茹), Wang Lin escorts 周茹 to
    26→ * the Kunxu Realm (昆虚界) and entrusts her to 慕冰媚 (Mu Bingmei) for
    27→ * cultivation. This is the bridge between CRON-110 (soul transfer at
    28→ * Vermilion Bird Capital) and CRON-111 (cultivation growth service, which
    29→ * requires 周茹 to be near Mu Bingmei at the Kunxu Realm).
    30→ *
    31→ * <h2>Canon Basis (fact-checked via web-search 2026-07-26)</h2>
    32→ * <p>In the novel 仙逆 (Renegade Immortal) by 耳根:
    33→ * <ul>
    34→ *   <li>After Wang Lin places Li Muwan's soul into 周茹 (CRON-110), 周茹
    35→ *       eventually becomes a Soul Transformation (炼虚) cultivator under
    36→ *       慕冰媚 (Mu Bingmei) in the Kunxu Realm (昆虚界).</li>
    37→ *   <li>The novel does not dwell on the logistics of how 周茹 travels to
    38→ *       the Kunxu Realm. The Kunxu Realm is canonically a pocket-realm
    39→ *       accessible from the Cave World — the journey is implied rather
    40→ *       than narrated beat-by-beat.</li>
    41→ *   <li>The disciple-master relationship between 周茹 and Mu Bingmei is
    42→ *       canon-attested: Mu Bingmei takes 周茹 as her disciple (RICanonicalDatabase
    43→ *       N19 + L74 + N10).</li>
    44→ *   <li>Wang Lin's role in placing 周茹 under Mu Bingmei's care is the
    45→ *       narrative bridge — he entrusts his "niece" (周茹 calls him
    46→ *       王林叔叔) to his third wife (Mu Bingmei) for cultivation.</li>
    47→ * </ul>
    48→ *
    49→ * <p><b>Canon honesty:</b> The novel clearly establishes (1) the soul
    50→ * transfer to 周茹 as Wang Lin's first revival attempt (CRON-110),
    51→ * (2) Mu Bingmei as 周茹's master in the Kunxu Realm, (3) 周茹's eventual
    52→ * cultivation to Soul Transformation. The exact chapter of the journey
    53→ * is not cited here to avoid fabrication. Sources: Baidu Baike 周茹,
    54→ * RICanonicalDatabase entry N10 (Zhou Ru), N19 (Mu Bingmei), L74 (Kunxu
    55→ * Realm), Fandom wiki.
    56→ *
    57→ * <h2>Trigger</h2>
    58→ * <p>Called from {@link dev.ergenverse.history.HistoryEvents#onEntityInteract}
    59→ * when a ServerPlayer right-clicks an EntityCultivator whose characterId
    60→ * equals {@value #CHARACTER_ID}, AND the player's main-hand bead already
    61→ * has {@link HeavenDefyingBeadItem#hasSoulTransferredToZhouRu} == true
    62→ * (i.e., CRON-110's soul transfer has already fired on a prior right-click).
    63→ *
    64→ * <p>The handler implements the following gates:
    65→ * <ol>
    66→ *   <li><b>Same-tick guard.</b> If the soul transfer fired on THIS tick
    67→ *       (i.e., {@code level.getGameTime() == soul_transfer_tick}), no-op.
    68→ *       This prevents the departure from firing on the same right-click
    69→ *       as the transfer — the player must right-click 周茹 a second time
    70→ *       to send her to the Kunxu Realm. Canon-faithful: the transfer
    71→ *       and the journey are distinct narrative beats.</li>
    72→ *   <li><b>Write-once guard.</b> If 周茹's runtime state already has
    73→ *       {@code "sent_to_kunxu": true}, no-op with a canon-faithful
    74→ *       "already departed" message. The departure is a one-time event
    75→ *       per save.</li>
    76→ *   <li><b>CRON-110 prerequisite.</b> If the soul transfer flag is
    77→ *       absent from 周茹's runtime state (e.g., world was reset, NPC
    78→ *       state was cleared), the departure cannot fire. Defensive — the
    79→ *       caller gates on the bead's {@code hasSoulTransferredToZhouRu}
    80→ *       flag, but this double-checks the runtime state.</li>
    81→ * </ol>
    82→ *
    83→ * <p>If all gates pass: teleports 周茹 to {@link PlanetSuzakuBlueprint#KUNXU_REALM}
    84→ * (-3500, surface, -3500), sets the {@code "sent_to_kunxu": true} flag in
    85→ * her runtime state, spawns canon-faithful particle/sound effects at both
    86→ * the origin (departure burst) and the destination (arrival burst),
    87→ * displays a bilingual message, and records the event in
    88→ * {@link HistoryManager#onDiscovery}.
    89→ *
    90→ * <h2>Why a dedicated class (not inline in HistoryEvents)</h2>
    91→ * <p>Mirrors CRON-99's {@link LiMuwanSoulCaptureEvent} and CRON-110's
    92→ * {@link ZhouRuSoulTransferEvent} — a dedicated static-utility class keeps
    93→ * the event listener clean and makes the departure logic independently
    94→ * testable. The class lives in the {@code wanglin.bead} package because
    95→ * the departure is gated on the bead's {@code hasSoulTransferredToZhouRu}
    96→ * flag (the bead is the canon-faithful state record of the transfer).
    97→ *
    98→ * <h2>Teleportation Safety</h2>
    99→ * <p>The destination chunk at (-3500, -3500) is typically unloaded when
   100→ * the departure fires (the player is near 周茹 at Vermilion Bird Capital,
   101→ * ~5000 blocks away). The handler:
   102→ * <ol>
   103→ *   <li>Force-loads the destination chunk via
   104→ *       {@link ServerLevel#getChunk(int, int)} (synchronous load).</li>
   105→ *   <li>Queries the surface Y via
   106→ *       {@link ServerLevel#getHeightmapPos(Heightmap.Types#MOTION_BLOCKING, BlockPos)}
   107→ *       to find a safe teleport Y.</li>
   108→ *   <li>Teleports 周茹 via {@link EntityCultivator#teleportTo(double, double, double)}.</li>
   109→ * </ol>
   110→ * If the destination is in an unloaded chunk after teleport, the
   111→ * {@link dev.ergenverse.runtime.materialize.CanonActorMaterializer}
   112→ * will dematerialize 周茹 on chunk unload and rematerialize her when the
   113→ * player next loads the area. The canon UUID persistence ensures 周茹
   114→ * is the same entity across dematerialize/rematerialize cycles.
   115→ *
   116→ * <h2>State Transition Diagram</h2>
   117→ * <pre>
   118→ *   CRON-99:  Li Muwan dies  →  bead.hasLiMuwanSoul = true
   119→ *   CRON-110: Player interacts with Zhou Ru (1st right-click)

   121→ *             →  Zhou Ru runtime: pregnant_with_li_muwan_soul = true
   122→ *             →  Zhou Ru runtime: soul_transfer_tick = currentTick
   123→ *   CRON-112: Player interacts with Zhou Ru (2nd right-click, later tick)
   124→ *             →  Zhou Ru teleports to KUNXU_REALM (-3500, surface, -3500)
   125→ *             →  Zhou Ru runtime: sent_to_kunxu = true
   126→ *             →  Zhou Ru runtime: kunxu_departure_tick = currentTick
   127→ *   CRON-111: Zhou Ru near Mu Bingmei (daily tick) → Zhou Ru realm advances
   128→ *             mortal → qi_condensation → ... → soul_transformation (CANON_CAP)
   129→ *   CRON-100: Player invokes /ergenverse bead revive → revivalAttempts++
   130→ *   CRON-102: 137th attempt with World Origin Essence → bead.isLiMuwanRevived = true
   131→ * </pre>

   133→ * <h2>Single-Player Maximalism (Article XLIII)</h2>

   135→ * parameter is the sole candidate. There is exactly one 周茹 (canon UUID
   136→ * {@link dev.ergenverse.runtime.CanonUUID#ZHOU_RU}). The departure is a
   137→ * one-time event per save. After departure, 周茹 persists at the Kunxu
   138→ * Realm across save/load cycles via CanonActorMaterializer + WorldRuntimeState.
   139→ *
   140→ * <p>MC 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.0 / Java 17.
   141→ *

   143→ * @see ZhouRuCultivationGrowthService (successor — CRON-111)

   145→ * @see dev.ergenverse.runtime.CanonUUID#ZHOU_RU
   146→ * @see dev.ergenverse.runtime.CanonUUID#MU_BINGMEI

   148→ */

   174→    private ZhouRuKunxuDepartureEvent() {}
   175→
   176→    /**
   177→     * Handle the Kunxu departure interaction. Called from
   178→     * {@link dev.ergenverse.history.HistoryEvents#onEntityInteract} when
   179→     * the player right-clicks an EntityCultivator whose characterId equals
   180→     * {@value #CHARACTER_ID} AND the player's main-hand bead already has
   181→     * {@link HeavenDefyingBeadItem#hasSoulTransferredToZhouRu} == true.
   182→     *
   183→     * <p>This method is the SOLE caller that sets the {@code "sent_to_kunxu"}
   184→     * runtime state flag. Before CRON-112, there was no path for 周茹 to
   185→     * reach the Kunxu Realm — the player had to physically herd her across
   186→     * thousands of blocks. CRON-112 closes this gap with a canon-faithful
   187→     * "Wang Lin escorts 周茹 to the Kunxu Realm" quest step.
   188→     *
   189→     * @param serverPlayer the player who right-clicked 周茹 (Wang Lin)
   190→     * @param zhouRu       the EntityCultivator whose characterId is "zhou_ru"
   191→     */
   192→    public static void handleDeparture(ServerPlayer serverPlayer,
   193→                                        EntityCultivator zhouRu) {
   194→        // Defensive: never run on client side.
   195→        if (serverPlayer.level().isClientSide()) return;
   196→
   197→        // Defensive: validate the target's characterId. The caller
   198→        // (HistoryEvents) gates on this, but defensive validation is
   199→        // cheap and prevents subtle bugs if the caller changes.
   200→        if (!CHARACTER_ID.equals(zhouRu.getCharacterId())) {
   201→            Ergenverse.LOGGER.warn("[Ergenverse] CRON-112: ZhouRuKunxuDepartureEvent "
   202→                    + "invoked with wrong characterId='{}'. Expected '{}'. No-op.",
   203→                    zhouRu.getCharacterId(), CHARACTER_ID);
   204→            return;
   205→        }
   206→
   207→        ServerLevel serverLevel = (ServerLevel) serverPlayer.level();
   208→
   209→        // ── 1. Defensive: re-validate the bead is in main hand and the
   210→        // transfer flag is set. The caller (HistoryEvents) gates on this,
   211→        // but defensive validation prevents subtle bugs. ──
   212→        ItemStack mainHand = serverPlayer.getItemInHand(InteractionHand.MAIN_HAND);
   213→        if (mainHand.isEmpty() || !(mainHand.getItem() instanceof HeavenDefyingBeadItem beadItem)) {
   214→            // No bead in main hand — this branch should never fire because
   215→            // the caller dispatches to the transfer handler (CRON-110) when
   216→            // there's no bead or no transfer flag. Included for robustness.
   217→            announceNoBead(serverPlayer);
   218→            return;
   219→        }
   220→        if (!beadItem.hasSoulTransferredToZhouRu(mainHand)) {
   221→            // Defensive: the transfer flag is not set. The caller should
   222→            // have dispatched to the transfer handler instead. No-op.
   223→            Ergenverse.LOGGER.warn("[Ergenverse] CRON-112: ZhouRuKunxuDepartureEvent "
   224→                    + "invoked but bead.hasSoulTransferredToZhouRu is false. No-op.");
   225→            return;
   226→        }
   227→
   228→        // ── 2. Fetch 周茹's runtime state ──
   229→        CompoundTag zhouRuState;
   230→        try {
   231→            WorldRuntimeState runtime = WorldRuntimeState.get(serverLevel);
   232→            zhouRuState = runtime.getNpcState(zhouRu.getCharacterId());
   233→            if (zhouRuState == null) zhouRuState = new CompoundTag();
   234→        } catch (Throwable t) {
   235→            Ergenverse.LOGGER.warn("[Ergenverse] CRON-112: failed to fetch Zhou Ru's "
   236→                    + "runtime state: {}", t.getMessage());
   237→            announceInternalError(serverPlayer);
   238→            return;
   239→        }
   240→
   241→        // ── 3. CRON-110 prerequisite: pregnant_with_li_muwan_soul must be true ──
   242→        // Defensive: the bead's hasSoulTransferredToZhouRu flag is the canonical
   243→        // record, but the runtime state should also have the flag set by CRON-110.
   244→        // If the runtime state is missing the flag (e.g., world was reset), the
   245→        // departure cannot fire — we cannot teleport 周茹 to the Kunxu Realm
   246→        // without the canon-faithful soul-transfer prerequisite.
   247→        if (!zhouRuState.getBoolean("pregnant_with_li_muwan_soul")) {
   248→            announceNoSoulTransferred(serverPlayer);
   249→            return;
   250→        }
   251→
   252→        // ── 4. Write-once guard: sent_to_kunxu must be false ──
   253→        // The departure is a one-time event per save. Calling this a second
   254→        // time is a no-op with a canon-faithful "already departed" message.
   255→        if (zhouRuState.getBoolean("sent_to_kunxu")) {
   256→            announceAlreadyDeparted(serverPlayer);
   257→            return;
   258→        }
   259→
   260→        // ── 5. Same-tick guard: departure must fire on a DIFFERENT right-click
   261→        // than the soul transfer. ──
   262→        // CRON-110 records soul_transfer_tick in 周茹's runtime state. If the
   263→        // current tick equals that value, the transfer fired on THIS right-click
   264→        // — the player has not yet had a chance to read the transfer message.
   265→        // We require the departure to fire on a SUBSEQUENT right-click.
   266→        long currentTick = serverLevel.getGameTime();
   267→        long soulTransferTick = zhouRuState.getLong("soul_transfer_tick");
   268→        if (currentTick == soulTransferTick) {
   269→            // Same-tick — silently no-op. The player will right-click again
   270→            // on a subsequent tick and the departure will fire then.
   271→            // We do NOT print a message here — the CRON-110 transfer message
   272→            // has already been displayed this tick.
   273→            return;
   274→        }
   275→
   276→        // ── 6. All gates pass — execute the Kunxu departure ──
   277→        // Record the origin position BEFORE teleporting (for the departure burst).
   278→        double originX = zhouRu.getX();
   279→        double originY = zhouRu.getY();
   280→        double originZ = zhouRu.getZ();
   281→
   282→        // Compute the destination surface position.
   283→        BlockPos destBlockPos = new BlockPos(
   284→                PlanetSuzakuBlueprint.KUNXU_REALM.x,
   285→                PlanetSuzakuBlueprint.KUNXU_REALM.y,
   286→                PlanetSuzakuBlueprint.KUNXU_REALM.z);
   287→
   288→        // Force-load the destination chunk so the heightmap query is accurate
   289→        // and the teleport destination is safe.
   290→        serverLevel.getChunk(destBlockPos.getX() >> 4, destBlockPos.getZ() >> 4);
   291→
   292→        // Query the surface Y via MOTION_BLOCKING heightmap (highest non-
   293→        // motion-blocking block: ignores air, leaves, etc.; returns the
   294→        // first solid block from the top).
   295→        BlockPos surfacePos = serverLevel.getHeightmapPos(
   296→                Heightmap.Types.MOTION_BLOCKING, destBlockPos);
   297→
   298→        double destX = surfacePos.getX() + 0.5;
   299→        double destY = surfacePos.getY();
   300→        double destZ = surfacePos.getZ() + 0.5;
   301→
   302→        // ── 7. Spawn departure burst at origin (before teleport) ──
   303→        spawnDepartureBurst(serverLevel, originX, originY, originZ);
   304→
   305→        // ── 8. Teleport 周茹 to the Kunxu Realm surface ──
   306→        // Use the simple same-dimension teleportTo(x, y, z). 周茹 is an
   307→        // EntityCultivator (extends Mob → LivingEntity → Entity), so this
   308→        // method is available.
   309→        zhouRu.teleportTo(destX, destY, destZ);
   310→
   311→        // ── 9. Spawn arrival burst at destination (after teleport) ──
   312→        spawnArrivalBurst(serverLevel, destX, destY, destZ);
   313→
   314→        // ── 10. Set the sent_to_kunxu flag in 周茹's runtime state ──
   315→        zhouRuState.putBoolean("sent_to_kunxu", true);
   316→        zhouRuState.putLong("kunxu_departure_tick", currentTick);
   317→        zhouRuState.putInt("kunxu_x", surfacePos.getX());
   318→        zhouRuState.putInt("kunxu_y", surfacePos.getY());
   319→        zhouRuState.putInt("kunxu_z", surfacePos.getZ());
   320→        try {
   321→            WorldRuntimeState runtime = WorldRuntimeState.get(serverLevel);
   322→            runtime.updateNpcState(zhouRu.getCharacterId(), zhouRuState);
   323→        } catch (Throwable t) {
   324→            Ergenverse.LOGGER.warn("[Ergenverse] CRON-112: failed to persist Zhou Ru's "
   325→                    + "sent_to_kunxu flag: {}", t.getMessage());
   326→        }
   327→
   328→        // ── 11. Display canon-faithful bilingual message ──
   329→        announceDepartureSuccess(serverPlayer, zhouRu);
   330→
   331→        // ── 12. Record in HistoryManager ──
   332→        HistoryManager.onDiscovery(serverPlayer,
   333→                "zhou_ru_kunxu_departure",
   334→                "Wang Lin sent Zhou Ru (周茹) to the Kunxu Realm (昆虚界) at "
   335→                        + "(" + surfacePos.getX() + ", " + surfacePos.getY() + ", "
   336→                        + surfacePos.getZ() + "), entrusting her to Mu Bingmei (慕冰媚) "
   337→                        + "for cultivation. The Li Muwan soul within her begins its "
   338→                        + "long cultivation arc toward Soul Transformation.",
   339→                serverLevel.getGameTime());
   340→
   341→        Ergenverse.LOGGER.info("[Ergenverse] CRON-112: Zhou Ru (周茹) departed for the "
   342→                        + "Kunxu Realm at tick {}. Origin: ({:.1f}, {:.1f}, {:.1f}). "
   343→                        + "Destination: ({:.1f}, {:.1f}, {:.1f}). Player: {}. "
   344→                        + "sent_to_kunxu=true recorded in runtime state.",
   345→                currentTick,
   346→                originX, originY, originZ,
   347→                destX, destY, destZ,
   348→                serverPlayer.getName().getString());
   349→    }
   350→
   351→    /**
   352→     * Spawn the departure burst at 周茹's origin position. The visual idiom:
   353→     * <ul>
   354→     *   <li><b>END_ROD particles in an ascending ring</b> — the soul-stream
   355→     *       rising as 周茹 departs. END_ROD is bright white-rising.</li>
   356→     *   <li><b>DRAGON_BREATH particles in a magenta burst</b> — the rift
   357→     *       tear opening. DRAGON_BREATH is purple-magenta, matching the
   358→     *       teleportation-rift visual idiom.</li>
   359→     *   <li><b>END_ROD particles in a vertical stream</b> — 周茹's form
   360→     *       ascending into the rift.</li>
   361→     *   <li><b>ENDERMAN_TELEPORT sound</b> — the classic teleport sound.</li>
   362→     *   <li><b>AMETHYST_BLOCK_CHIME sound at high pitch</b> — the bright
   363→     *       crystalline tone of the bead's release.</li>
   364→     * </ul>
   365→     */
   366→    private static void spawnDepartureBurst(ServerLevel serverLevel,
   367→                                              double x, double y, double z) {
   368→        BlockPos pos = BlockPos.containing(x, y, z);
   369→
   370→        // ── Ascending ring of END_ROD around 周茹 ──
   371→        for (int i = 0; i < DEPARTURE_RING_PARTICLE_COUNT; i++) {
   372→            double theta = (i / (double) DEPARTURE_RING_PARTICLE_COUNT) * Math.PI * 2;
   373→            double radius = 0.9;
   374→            double dx = Math.cos(theta) * radius;
   375→            double dz = Math.sin(theta) * radius;
   376→            double dy = 1.0 + ((i % 4) * 0.5);  // chest height to 2.5 above feet
   377→            serverLevel.sendParticles(ParticleTypes.END_ROD,
   378→                    x + dx, y + dy, z + dz, 1,
   379→                    0.0, 0.05, 0.0, 0.0);
   380→        }
   381→
   382→        // ── DRAGON_BREATH burst (rift tear) ──
   383→        serverLevel.sendParticles(ParticleTypes.DRAGON_BREATH,
   384→                x, y + 1.0, z, RIFT_PARTICLE_COUNT,
   385→                0.8, 0.4, 0.8, 0.05);
   386→
   387→        // ── Vertical END_ROD stream above 周茹 (form ascending) ──
   388→        for (int i = 0; i < STREAM_PARTICLE_COUNT; i++) {
   389→            double dy = 2.0 + (i * 0.4);
   390→            double dx = (i % 3 - 1) * 0.1;
   391→            double dz = (i % 5 - 2) * 0.1;
   392→            serverLevel.sendParticles(ParticleTypes.END_ROD,
   393→                    x + dx, y + dy, z + dz, 1,
   394→                    0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0);
   395→        }
   396→
   397→        // ── Sounds ──
   398→        // ENDERMAN_TELEPORT: the classic teleport sound.
   399→        serverLevel.playSound(null, pos,
   400→                SoundEvents.ENDERMAN_TELEPORT, SoundSource.HOSTILE,
   401→                0.9F, 1.0F);
   402→        // AMETHYST_BLOCK_CHIME: bright crystalline — the bead's release.
   403→        serverLevel.playSound(null, pos,
   404→                SoundEvents.AMETHYST_BLOCK_CHIME, SoundSource.AMBIENT,
   405→                0.8F, 1.4F);
   406→
   407→        // ── Central flash ──
   408→        serverLevel.sendParticles(ParticleTypes.FIREWORK,
   409→                x, y + 1.0, z, 6,
   410→                0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 0.05);
   411→    }

   413→    /**
   414→     * Spawn the arrival burst at the Kunxu Realm destination. Mirrors the
   415→     * departure burst but uses fewer particles (the arrival is quieter
   416→     * than the departure — the rift closes gently).
   417→     */
   418→    private static void spawnArrivalBurst(ServerLevel serverLevel,
   419→                                            double x, double y, double z) {
   420→        BlockPos pos = BlockPos.containing(x, y, z);
   421→
   422→        // ── END_ROD descending ring (soul-stream descending into arrival) ──
   423→        for (int i = 0; i < ARRIVAL_PARTICLE_COUNT; i++) {
   424→            double theta = (i / (double) ARRIVAL_PARTICLE_COUNT) * Math.PI * 2;
   425→            double radius = 0.9;
   426→            double dx = Math.cos(theta) * radius;
   427→            double dz = Math.sin(theta) * radius;
   428→            double dy = 1.0 + ((i % 3) * 0.6);
   429→            serverLevel.sendParticles(ParticleTypes.END_ROD,
   430→                    x + dx, y + dy, z + dz, 1,
   431→                    0.0, 0.05, 0.0, 0.0);
   432→        }
   433→
   434→        // ── DRAGON_BREATH small burst (rift closing) ──
   435→        serverLevel.sendParticles(ParticleTypes.DRAGON_BREATH,
   436→                x, y + 1.0, z, 12,
   437→                0.4, 0.2, 0.4, 0.03);
   438→
   439→        // ── Sound: ENDERMAN_TELEPORT (softer) ──
   440→        serverLevel.playSound(null, pos,
   441→                SoundEvents.ENDERMAN_TELEPORT, SoundSource.HOSTILE,
   442→                0.6F, 1.2F);
   443→    }
